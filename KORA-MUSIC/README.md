# KORA MUSIC

KORA MUSIC is the music discovery, artist and audio-content layer within the KORA ecosystem.

## Position

`KORA RADIO → KORA MUSIC → KORA ONE`

KORA MUSIC is designed to support African music first while remaining globally interoperable. It should be able to consume music and programme metadata from KORA RADIO without coupling the music catalogue to a particular streaming provider.

## Core domains

- Artists and profiles
- Releases, albums, EPs and singles
- Tracks and versions
- Playlists and stations
- Genres and territories
- Editorial programming
- Rights and availability metadata
- Audio previews and playback references
- Analytics and future monetisation

## Ownership principle

KORA owns the catalogue metadata, artist experience, discovery logic, business rules and control plane. External storage, CDN, audio processing, payment and distribution providers remain replaceable adapters.

## Commercial foundation

The model is designed to support future advertising, subscriptions, artist services, premium content, ticketing, merchandising and other lawful revenue streams without hard-coding a single monetisation provider.
