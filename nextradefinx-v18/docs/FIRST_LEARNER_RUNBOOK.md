# First Learner Runbook

The first real beta participant is a safety test, not a marketing launch.

Sequence:
1. V17 environment gate passes.
2. V17 two-user RLS isolation test passes with zero cross-user reads.
3. Invite exactly one learner.
4. Require verified email.
5. Collect versioned Terms, Privacy and Risk acknowledgements.
6. Confirm the beta is education + paper trading only.
7. Choose language, experience level and learning goal.
8. Create the Learning Passport.
9. Complete one guided lesson.
10. Complete one paper simulation.
11. Record one journal reflection.
12. Verify logout/login persistence.
13. Verify deletion-request flow without deleting until the test is complete.
14. Review incidents and support notes before inviting learner two.

Do not expand the cohort if there is any privacy, auth, persistence or consent defect.
