OUR ORIGINAL WORLD

Meet the Rainbow Town crew

Designed as an ownable character universe for episodes, songs, books, games, live shows and merchandise — without copying an existing cartoon's characters or stories.

Tumi

Curious, inventive and always asking “why?”. Loves drums, building things and counting.

ORIGINAL SING-ALONG

The Five Little Steps Song

Short, repetitive and movement-based so children can learn the routine by doing it.

One — wake up, stretch up high! ☀️
Two — brush brush, smile so bright! 🪥
Three — wash wash, splash splash splash! 💦
Four — clothes on, ready fast! 👕
Five — breakfast, clap and say:
“Good morning, Rainbow Town — hooray!” 🎵
SERIES ENGINE

Built to become a franchise

The show format can scale into a large library while keeping each episode simple, safe and recognizable for preschool viewers.

01

Learning adventures

Numbers, colours, shapes, animals, routines, road safety, emotions, food, nature and early language.

02

African + global world

Start in Rainbow Town and travel through Southern African environments, then expand with global friends and cultures.

03

Multi-format IP

Shorts, 7-minute episodes, 20-minute compilations, songs, bedtime stories, simple games, activity sheets and live events.

KORA KIDS · TUMI & TALA
Original concept prototype. Characters, setting, dialogue and songs are original to this project. The creative direction uses common preschool animation conventions such as musical repetition and educational storytelling, not another show's protected characters, artwork, music or scripts.
