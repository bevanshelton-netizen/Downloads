# KORA GOSPEL TV — Worldwide Operating Model

KORA GOSPEL TV is designed as a global Gospel network with **one IZAKHONO-owned control plane** and multiple regional distribution layers.

## Core rule

The global network may use any suitable external platform for reach, but editorial control, master programme state, distribution policy and the authoritative feed remain with IZAKHONO.

## Regionalisation

Regional playout does not require different ownership. It uses:
- regional programme clocks;
- regional sponsor inventory;
- regional rights windows;
- approved local church and artist contributions;
- region-specific captions, dubs and metadata;
- platform-specific distribution adapters.

## Language priorities

Phase-one global language work should prioritize English, French, Portuguese, Spanish, Swahili, isiZulu and isiXhosa. Phase two extends to Arabic, Hindi, Indonesian, Simplified Chinese, Yoruba and Hausa.

Translation may not change the meaning of testimony, scripture attribution, speaker identity, ministry attribution or rights metadata.

## Distribution

External distribution may include global video platforms, social platforms, FAST/CTV services, smart-TV stores, broadcasters, telecom bundles, churches and Christian media networks.

No territory should be described as live until the actual public route and any required platform/territory approvals have been verified.

## Compliance

Before targeted launch in a territory, confirm:
1. content and music rights cover that territory and medium;
2. local broadcasting/streaming requirements, if applicable;
3. advertising and sponsorship rules;
4. privacy/data handling obligations;
5. platform terms and account eligibility;
6. any restrictions applying specifically to religious broadcasting or fundraising.

The worldwide architecture is global by design, but territory activation is evidence-based rather than assumed.
