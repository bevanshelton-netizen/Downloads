PRACTICAL SME GUIDE

Buy, finance or hire? Test the cash impact first.

The cheapest-looking option is not always the best business decision. Consider utilisation, cash preservation, maintenance, obsolescence and the cost of being unable to operate.

✓ Estimate how often the asset will actually be used.
✓ Compare total cash cost across the expected useful period.
✓ Include maintenance, insurance and downtime risk.
✓ Protect working capital and minimum cash buffers.
✓ Do not buy something solely for a possible tax deduction.
