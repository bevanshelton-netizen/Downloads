Can your business afford another employee?

The real question is not only whether you can cover the salary this month. It is whether cash, payroll costs and the expected value created by the role remain sustainable over the next several weeks.

✓ Start with total employment cost, not take-home pay.
✓ Put the new payroll cost into a 13-week cash forecast.
✓ Stress-test slower customer payments and weaker sales.
✓ Define what revenue, capacity or risk reduction the role must create.
✓ Keep a minimum cash buffer after the hire.
