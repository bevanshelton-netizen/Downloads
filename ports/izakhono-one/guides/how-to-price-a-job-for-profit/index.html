PRACTICAL SME GUIDE

Stop quoting from instinct.

A profitable quote starts with the full cost of delivering the work, then adds the margin the business needs. Revenue is not profit.

✓ Capture direct materials and labour.
✓ Include delivery, subcontractor and job-specific costs.
✓ Account for overhead rather than pretending it is free.
✓ Apply a target gross margin before VAT/tax treatment.
✓ Check the final price against the customer's scope and risk.
