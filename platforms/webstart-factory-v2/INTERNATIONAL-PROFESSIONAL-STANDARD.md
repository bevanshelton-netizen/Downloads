# International Professional Deployment Standard

Effective immediately, WebStart must not publish a customer website merely because it is technically functional.

A site may publish only when:
- `quality_level = international-ready`
- the professional quality gate passes
- score is at least **95/100**
- every critical check passes
- public HTTPS verification succeeds after publish
- required payment has been completed

## Critical professionalism checks
- complete HTML document and language metadata
- mobile viewport and responsive CSS
- professional title and meta description
- Open Graph metadata
- structured Organization data
- strong H1 / positioning
- real navigation and core business sections
- at least three service propositions
- substantive About copy
- clear call to action
- real customer contact route
- working enquiry form
- accessible form labels
- professional footer
- no placeholder or unfinished copy
- no exposed secrets
- verified public HTML after launch

A site that fails the gate returns to review/draft and cannot be deployed publicly.

This rule applies to every WebStart-generated site regardless of package, sector, customer, or requested urgency.
