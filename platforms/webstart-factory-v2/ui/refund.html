Effective 12 September 2026. This operating policy does not override rights granted by applicable law.

1. Overview

WebStart combines automated digital services with setup, website generation and ongoing hosting.

Once personalised work begins, the setup component may no longer be fully refundable, subject to applicable consumer law.

Customers may request cancellation of future recurring charges; service continues through the applicable paid period unless otherwise agreed.

5. Service failure

If a paid service materially fails, IZAKHONO will first attempt correction, then consider an appropriate remedy where correction is not reasonably possible.

6. Statutory rights

This policy does not remove rights under the Consumer Protection Act or other applicable South African law.

7. Refund method

Approved refunds are ordinarily returned using the original payment route where practical.
