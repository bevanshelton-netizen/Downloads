# Learning Passport Privacy Boundary

The Learning Passport is intentionally narrower than a financial-services customer profile.

It may store language preference, learning experience level, selected educational goal, lesson/quiz progress, paper-trading practice events, journaling completion and readiness snapshots.

It must not store bank credentials, card data, OTPs, live brokerage passwords, real account balances, live portfolio holdings or unnecessary identity documents.

Any future regulated KYC, brokerage suitability or advice-related data must live behind a separate regulated partner/compliance boundary and must not be silently inferred from educational progress.

Public-facing progress views must use redacted learner data and must not expose user identifiers or raw journals.
