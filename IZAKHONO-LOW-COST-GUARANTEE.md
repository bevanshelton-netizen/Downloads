# IZAKHONO LOW-COST GUARANTEE

## Rule

For every directly comparable paid Microsoft or Google product, IZAKHONO targets a **lower customer price** and a **lower total cost of ownership**.

This is a design constraint, not a marketing slogan.

## Default target

- Target price: **70% or less** of the comparable incumbent paid plan where the capability is equivalent.
- Hard ceiling: **95%** of the comparable incumbent price. If we cannot beat that sustainably, we optimise the architecture before calling the product commercially ready.
- A useful free tier is required wherever compute economics allow it.
- Consumer AI should not default to per-message billing.

## How we keep prices lower

1. Owned infrastructure first.
2. Owned GPU inference first.
3. One shared IZAKHONO ACCOUNT / DATA / AI / SECURITY / PAY stack.
4. No duplicate infrastructure per app.
5. Open formats and low migration friction.
6. External providers only for reversible overflow/fallback.
7. No behavioural-advertising subsidy that depends on tracking users.
8. Cost-per-active-user and cost-per-inference must be measured continuously.

## Benchmarking

Competitor pricing must be fetched from current public Microsoft and Google pricing sources at pricing-review time. Static historical prices must not be treated as current.

Each comparable product records:
- incumbent product and plan;
- incumbent public price;
- IZAKHONO equivalent;
- IZAKHONO target price;
- feature-parity notes;
- infrastructure cost per active user;
- margin headroom.

## Commercial gate

If we cannot sustainably undercut the comparable incumbent, the product stays in `building` or `pilot`. We do not hide an unsustainable cost behind the word "unlimited".

## Bundle strategy

The customer should pay less because one subscription can replace several separate services.

- IZAKHONO ONE Free
- IZAKHONO ONE
- IZAKHONO ONE Creator
- IZAKHONO ONE Business
- IZAKHONO ONE Education
