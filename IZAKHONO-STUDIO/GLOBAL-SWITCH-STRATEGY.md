# IZAKHONO CREATIVE SUITE — GLOBAL SWITCH STRATEGY

## Core principle

**Switching must be easier than staying.**

The $5 price opens the door, but price alone will not create durable adoption. The product must remove the four biggest switching costs: files, habits, collaboration and trust.

## Product-led switch system

### 1. Switch Center
- Inventory a user's existing creative files in one browser flow.
- Preserve originals.
- Accept common formats first.
- Identify proprietary/source formats that need conversion instead of pretending they are fully supported.
- Generate a migration manifest so nothing disappears silently.
- Build native/compatible importers in measured waves.

### 2. No lock-in promise
- Common-format export must stay first-class.
- Users keep original uploads/source files.
- Avoid subscription traps that make leaving difficult.
- Make projects portable across web, desktop wrapper and owned infrastructure.

### 3. One-project universe
A logo made in Vector should be usable in Canvas, Layout, Cut and Motion without repeated exports. Brand kits, fonts, assets and approvals should be shared.

### 4. Global-first localisation
- Major world languages.
- African languages built in early rather than appended later.
- RTL support.
- Local templates, dimensions and cultural packs.
- Currency-local checkout while keeping the global USD $5 reference price where lawful and operationally supported.

### 5. Performance as a growth weapon
- Browser-first.
- Local-first editing where practical.
- Progressive enhancement for weaker machines/connections.
- Proxy media for video.
- Explicit cloud/offline state.

## Acquisition programme

### Creator switch campaign
Message: **"Bring your work. Keep your files. Pay $5. Create in one place."**

### Small business wedge
Bundle:
- design;
- photo;
- social resizing;
- campaign generation;
- website/brand asset handoff.

This creates a reason to use IZAKHONO daily, not only for occasional creative work.

### Education wedge
Provide institution onboarding, labs/classroom deployment, template libraries and curriculum-friendly workflows. Do not promise special education pricing until approved.

### Agencies and teams
Prioritise:
- brand kits;
- review links;
- comments;
- approvals;
- shared assets;
- role permissions;
- client export packs.

## Viral loop

Every exported project may offer an optional, non-intrusive "Created with IZAKHONO" share attribution. Never force a watermark on paid outputs.

Referral mechanics should be introduced only after subscription billing is proven end-to-end. Any free-month/reward economics must be modelled against churn, payment fees and AI compute first.

## Trust requirements

- Clear ownership terms for user content.
- No silent training on customer assets.
- No hidden telemetry.
- Transparent AI provider use.
- Explicit network activity.
- Clear security and backup posture.
- FORTRESS controls for secrets, service health and audit evidence.

## Competitive advantage to protect

Do not compete only on feature count. Protect these differentiators:

1. $5 one-core-suite simplicity.
2. Migration tooling built into the product.
3. One shared creative project universe.
4. Local-first performance and privacy.
5. Portfolio-scale campaign automation.
6. African + global localisation.
7. Owned-first infrastructure with reversible fallback.
8. Open/common export to avoid lock-in.

## Launch gates

Do not run mass acquisition until:
- a public route is verified;
- sign-up/auth is stable;
- the $5 product is registered in the selected gateway;
- legal/privacy/refund terms are published;
- end-to-end payment and cancellation are tested;
- Switch Center and core editors survive real user test files;
- backups/restore and rollback pass;
- support and abuse-reporting paths exist.
