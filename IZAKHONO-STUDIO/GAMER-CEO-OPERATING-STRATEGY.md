# IZAKHONO GAMER — CEO OPERATING STRATEGY

## Product position
USD $15/month. Includes the USD $5 Creative Suite plus Gamer Studio.

The product wins by becoming useful after every gaming session, not by merely offering more design templates.

## Activation loop
1. Import gameplay or create a gamer asset.
2. Mark a moment or build an overlay/thumbnail.
3. Export locally.
4. Save a gamer/team project.
5. Return after the next session.

## Margin protection
- Local rendering is the default for supported browser workflows.
- Clip Lab v1 caps local rendered clips at 60 seconds.
- Remote/cloud rendering is not included as unlimited compute in the $15 plan.
- Future remote rendering, expensive AI generation and large hosted storage require measured allowances/top-ups or higher plans.
- Never silently turn the $15 plan into an unlimited GPU/storage promise.

## Retention features
- Portable gamer projects.
- Saved team/channel presets.
- Clip moment markers.
- Cross-format outputs for YouTube, Shorts/Reels/TikTok and feed.
- Sponsor fields for esports/team commercialisation.

## Launch funnel
Public landing → signup → $15 checkout → first export → saved project → repeat creator session.

## Internal operating metrics
These are initial management targets, to be revised with real cohort data:
- First-session activation: at least 50% of paid users complete one export.
- Checkout completion: monitor by market/payment method; remove avoidable payment friction.
- 7-day return rate: target at least 35% after onboarding is mature.
- Local export success: target above 95% on supported browsers/formats.
- Gross margin: protect above 70% by keeping heavy compute metered.
- Refund/support causes: classify weekly and feed directly into product fixes.

## Marketing rule
The prepared global campaign remains HOLD until public HTTPS, signup/auth, $15 checkout, legal/refund/cancellation and reconciliation gates are verified. Once they pass, owned media goes first; authorised organic external publishing follows. Paid media requires a separately approved budget.
