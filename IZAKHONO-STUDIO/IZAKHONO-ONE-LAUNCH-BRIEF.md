# IZAKHONO ONE — Launch Campaign Brief

## Campaign
**Switch to ONE**

## Primary objective
Acquire the first **1,000 activated founding accounts** through owned media, referrals, portfolio cross-promotion and partners before scaling paid acquisition.

## Core proposition
**One account. One AI. Everything.**

IZAKHONO ONE is positioned as a privacy-first, owned-first, lower-cost alternative to fragmented digital subscriptions.

## Audience
Individuals, small businesses, educators, creators and developers who are paying for multiple disconnected tools or want more control over their data.

## Creative pillars
1. **Stop overpaying** — one ecosystem instead of multiple separate subscriptions.
2. **No behavioural tracking** — privacy is a product feature, not a paid add-on.
3. **One account** — move between AI, productivity, media, education, business and developer tools without separate identity silos.
4. **Owned-first AI** — local/owned inference first, reversible overflow when necessary.
5. **Bring your work** — switching/import/export should be easier than staying locked in.
6. **Built in Africa for the world** — global ambition with African language/local relevance.

## CTA hierarchy
- Join the Founding 1,000
- Open IZAKHONO ONE
- Invite someone who should stop overpaying
- Bring your work. Keep control.

## Launch sequence
Day 1: Why ONE exists  
Day 2: The fragmentation/cost problem  
Day 3: Privacy and no profiling  
Day 4: One account across the ecosystem  
Day 5: ONE AI and owned compute  
Day 6: Switching/import/export  
Day 7: Founding-member referral push

## Channel order
Owned portfolio placements first: IZAKHONO ONE, KORA, Edu-Build, FAISReady, WorkNow, LegacyMart and portfolio email.

Then organic: LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, SEO, community groups and press.

Paid campaigns remain paused/approval-gated until activation, D7/D30 retention and cost-per-active-account are measurable.

## Price communication
Do not publish final IZAKHONO ONE commercial prices until the cost-proof gate is green.

Internal launch targets:
- Free: R0 / US$0
- Individual: US$3.50/month target
- Business entry: US$4.50/user/month target
- Education: US$2/user/month target at institutional scale

These are targets, not public promises.

## Measurement
North star: weekly active accounts using two or more IZAKHONO capabilities.

Supporting measures: activation, D7/D30 retention, activated referrals/member, partner activations, AI cost/active user, gross margin, support rate, successful task rate, uptime and latency.

## Privacy
Referral attribution happens only at account registration/activation. No tracking pixel, behavioural advertising profile or cross-site referral cookie.
