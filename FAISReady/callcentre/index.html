INTEGRATION MODEL

Telephony stays modular

FAISReady should own contacts, campaigns, learner/candidate linkage, QA, outcomes, permissions and reporting. Voice transport can be provided by a configurable SIP/VoIP/contact-centre provider.

Planned interfaces: inbound webhook, outbound dial request, call event stream, recording reference, transcription result and disposition sync.

South African governance first.
Call recording, consent, POPIA, direct marketing, employment assessment fairness and any regulated advice/disclosure workflows must be configured for the institution’s lawful use case. This module does not itself provide financial advice or official regulatory certification.
