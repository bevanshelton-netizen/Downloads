# IZAKHONO ONE — CEO Growth Strategy

## Executive direction

IZAKHONO ONE will not try to outspend Microsoft or Google. It will grow by making the product **cheaper, easier to switch to, more private and more integrated**, then turning product usage into distribution.

The central proposition is:

**One account. One AI. Everything.**

The company should use a product-led growth model first, then add partnerships and paid acquisition only after the unit economics are visible.

## Market benchmark

As checked on 23 September 2026, Google Workspace's public South Africa-facing pricing shows Business Starter at US$7/user/month, Standard at US$14 and Plus at US$22 on annual commitment. Microsoft 365's South Africa-facing pricing shows Business Basic at US$7/user/month, Standard at US$14 and Premium at US$22 with Teams. Microsoft's no-Teams annual plans start at US$5.40/user/month.

The initial IZAKHONO ONE target is therefore:
- useful Free tier;
- Individual target: US$3.50/month;
- Business entry target: US$4.50/user/month;
- Education target: US$2/user/month at institutional scale.

These are **cost targets, not yet public commercial promises**. Final pricing only becomes public after owned inference, storage, support and payment costs prove the target is sustainable.

## Growth sequence

**Founding 1,000.** Acquire the first 1,000 activated users through the existing IZAKHONO portfolio, referrals, founder-led content, partners, campuses and owned media. Do not burn cash on broad paid advertising.

**Product-market proof.** Measure activation, D7/D30 retention, weekly use across two or more IZAKHONO capabilities, referral conversion and cost per active account.

**Scale.** Once economics are proven, expand education/business licensing, reseller/partner channels and only then profitable paid media.

## Distribution engine

Every activated member receives a personal invite link. Referral attribution is only attached to account registration/activation; there is no behavioural tracking cookie and no cross-site profile.

Every public IZAKHONO surface should expose a native Share action. Portfolio products should route users toward ONE Account instead of creating new identity silos.

IZAKHONO Studio produces the creative assets. Growth OS creates the plan, localisation, approval workflow and channel pack. Paid writes remain approval-gated.

## Core channels

Owned distribution comes first: ONE launchpad, KORA, Edu-Build, FAISReady, WorkNow, LegacyMart, portfolio email and cross-promotion.

Organic distribution is founder-led and product-led: LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, SEO, community groups, press and partner newsletters.

Partnership distribution should prioritise education/training networks, SMEs, BPO/GBS, banks/insurers, creative organisations, resellers and campus partners.

## CEO dashboard

The north-star metric is **weekly active accounts using at least two IZAKHONO capabilities**.

The board/CEO dashboard should always show activation, D7/D30 retention, referrals per active user, partner-sourced activations, AI cost per active account, gross margin, CAC, payback, successful task rate, support rate, uptime and latency.

## Non-negotiables

No behavioural tracking. No fake scarcity. No broad paid advertising before economics are measured. No final public pricing before cost proof. No “live” claims without verification. No referral system that becomes a surveillance system.
