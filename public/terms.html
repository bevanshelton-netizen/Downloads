Founding Beta Platform Terms

Version 2026-08-29. Provisional launch terms; formal legal review is required before scaled public monetisation.

VIDEONOMY is currently a controlled founding beta. Features may change, be limited or be withdrawn while the product is being tested.

Revenue-share statements

Displayed percentages are platform target shares for eligible revenue categories. They are not a promise of a fixed amount per view or a guarantee that any content will earn money. Eligibility, advertiser demand, payment-processing costs, taxes, rights claims, fraud adjustments and applicable law may affect actual amounts.

Advertising offers

Founding advertising and sponsorship packages are proposals subject to acceptance and written confirmation. Unless expressly agreed in writing, beta packages do not guarantee a minimum number of impressions, views, conversions or sales.

Acceptable use

Users may not misuse the service, interfere with security, inflate views, violate intellectual-property rights, distribute unlawful material or use the platform to exploit or endanger others.

Availability

The beta is provided on a best-effort basis and may experience interruptions. Production-grade service levels will be introduced as the platform graduates from bootstrap infrastructure.
