VIDEONOMY Creator Terms

Version 2026-09-25. Release-candidate operating terms; formal legal review remains required before scaled public monetisation.

Your content

You must own or have sufficient rights to upload the content. You retain ownership of your content, while granting VIDEONOMY the permissions reasonably required to host, transmit, display, moderate, promote and technically process it for the service.

Founding beta

Initial creator access may be invitation-controlled. The current upload format is MP4 with a 90 MB per-file limit. Standard videos and Shorts share the creator account.

Creator economics

The platform currently targets 70% of eligible watch-page ad revenue, 80% of the subscription viewing pool and 90% of eligible direct fan and brand-marketplace revenue for creators. These percentages do not guarantee earnings. Direct-fan revenue first appears as pending earnings. Actual external processing costs, taxes where applicable, fraud reversals and rights claims are accounted for before earnings become available for payout. A qualified view is an audience metric and does not itself create a fixed payment.

Integrity

Artificial views, self-dealing, bot traffic, payment fraud, rights abuse and attempts to manipulate creator earnings are prohibited and may result in withheld or reversed amounts and account suspension.

Moderation

Content may be limited or removed when required for safety, law, rights protection or platform integrity. Creators remain responsible for their uploads.
