WORKNOW SAFETY STANDARD

A job offer should not put you at risk.

WorkNow will vet both sides where the platform has enough evidence to do so, but no online platform can guarantee that every person or opportunity is genuine. Never send passwords, PINs or card details to an employer. Treat requests for unusual upfront payments as a warning sign.
