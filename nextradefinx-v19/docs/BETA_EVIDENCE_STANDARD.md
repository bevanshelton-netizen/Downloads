# NexTradeFinX V19 Beta Evidence Standard

Cohort growth requires proof, not enthusiasm.

Automatic STOP: privacy breach, cross-user access, auth bypass, privileged-secret exposure, consent-integrity failure, failed RLS isolation, or any open critical defect.

Review: RLS isolation, consent integrity, auth/persistence, deletion flow, incidents, critical/medium defects, activation, first-session completion, and support load.

Aggregate only minimum education/product-operation events. Never collect bank credentials, OTPs, brokerage passwords, or rank learners by simulated profit.

Sequence: 1 learner -> review -> up to 2 -> review -> up to 5 -> review -> up to 10.

GO only means the education + paper-trading beta may expand. Real-money features remain disabled.
