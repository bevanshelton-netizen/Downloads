# IZAKHONO EVERYTHING — Portfolio Doctrine

## Mission

Build an IZAKHONO-owned alternative for every core digital capability our users depend on, with a unified account and a unified AI layer.

The goal is not to make cosmetic clones. Each product must be able to stand on its own while also becoming more useful when connected to the rest of the IZAKHONO ecosystem.

## Non-negotiable ownership rule

1. Core product logic, user experience, data contracts and APIs are IZAKHONO-owned.
2. IZAKHONO ACCOUNT is the common identity layer.
3. IZAKHONO ONE AI is the common intelligence/orchestration layer.
4. IZAKHONO DATA is the preferred data/storage layer.
5. IZAKHONO CLOUD/RUNTIME/EDGE are the preferred execution and delivery layers.
6. IZAKHONO GPU COMPUTE is the preferred inference-capacity layer.
7. FORTRESS is the common trust/security layer.
8. IZAKHONO PAY is the common payment orchestration layer.
9. External providers are permitted for bootstrap, compatibility, overflow and disaster recovery only when they remain replaceable.
10. No product may be called live, unlimited, secure, private or production-ready unless that claim has been technically verified.

## "Better" must be measurable

Every replacement must target concrete improvements in:
- privacy: no tracking, behavioural profiling or advertising IDs;
- simplicity: fewer steps and one account;
- interoperability: common formats, import/export and no lock-in;
- African languages and local relevance;
- accessibility;
- cost efficiency;
- portability of data and content;
- cross-product workflows;
- reliability and recovery;
- owner/operator control.

## One account, one AI, many products

IZAKHONO ONE is the user-facing home and account layer.

IZAKHONO ONE AI is the intelligence layer inside every product. Apps do not get separate disconnected assistants. Each product exposes capabilities to ONE AI through a capability contract with explicit permissions.

## Build order

**Layer 0 — Infrastructure**
ACCOUNT → DATA → CODE → CLOUD/RUNTIME → QUEUE → API GATEWAY → GPU COMPUTE → FORTRESS → PAY.

**Layer 1 — Universal daily tools**
Search, Browser, Mail, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Calendar, Meet, Maps, Translate, Notes, Contacts.

**Layer 2 — Media and creativity**
KORA, Video, Radio, Photos, Creative Suite, Creator Studio.

**Layer 3 — Business and commerce**
LegacyMart, Shop Builder, CRM, Marketing, Accounting, Payroll, WorkNow, Edu-Build.

**Layer 4 — OS and devices**
IZAKHONO OS, app distribution, device sync and backup.

## Compatibility rule

We should make switching easier than staying. Where legally and technically possible, users must be able to import from incumbent services and export from IZAKHONO in standard formats.

## Capacity rule

Software can pool and schedule compute, but cannot create physical VRAM. Until owned GPUs are sufficient, external GPU nodes may join the same compute fabric as reversible overflow. The user-facing API must remain IZAKHONO-owned so providers can be replaced without changing every application.

## Privacy rule

Networked functionality must be explicit, necessary, visible and user-controlled. No silent telemetry. No behavioural advertising profile.

## Status rule

Every capability is one of: `foundation`, `building`, `pilot`, `verified-live`, or `planned`.

Architecture documents and AI responses must not imply that `planned` or `building` capabilities are already live.
