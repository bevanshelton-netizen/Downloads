## Full description
AUTO AI helps everyday motorists understand what their car may be telling them before they approve repairs or buy a used vehicle.

Use AUTO AI to:

• describe vehicle symptoms and warning lights in plain language;
• understand common diagnostic trouble codes without treating a code as a parts order;
• review a repair quotation for missing labour, testing or itemisation;
• screen a used car for common warning signs before purchase;
• see clear next checks and questions to ask a workshop or seller.

SAFETY FIRST

AUTO AI is decision support. It does not replace a qualified technician, physical inspection, roadworthy process or emergency assistance.

If you describe a safety-critical issue involving braking, steering, fire, fuel leakage, oil pressure, severe overheating or wheel security, AUTO AI prioritises stopping safely and seeking qualified help.

NO PARTS GUESSING

A diagnostic trouble code is evidence, not proof that a particular component must be replaced. AUTO AI is designed to help you ask better questions before expensive work is authorised.

LAUNCH EDITION

The first Google Play release focuses on core vehicle intelligence and does not require an AUTO AI account.
