## Portfolio execution cycle

Every product follows:

**BUILD -> VERIFY -> CONVERSION GATE -> GO LIVE -> CEO ROLLOUT -> MARKET -> MEASURE -> IMPROVE**

Ready products do not wait for unfinished products. Blocked products stay in completion lanes. Verified external routes stay active until owned public routes pass independent HTTPS/DNS/TLS/application verification.

## International public-site standard

Every public site must have:
- a clear value proposition and primary CTA above the fold;
- strong product-appropriate photography, illustration or original branded graphics;
- professional responsive typography, spacing and hierarchy;
- optimized media, accessible contrast and mobile-first usability;
- trustworthy contact/legal/privacy/refund/delivery surfaces where applicable;
- verified payment, enrolment, lead, quote or booking paths;
- SEO/social sharing metadata and professional error states;
- no fabricated testimonials, fake scarcity, unsupported claims, placeholder counters or dead CTAs.

- IZAKHONO CREATE as creative source of record;
