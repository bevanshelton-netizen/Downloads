# KORA NEWS

KORA NEWS is the news and public-information layer of KORA ONE.

## Architecture

`KORA CONNECT → KORA LIVE → KORA TV / KORA RADIO → KORA NEWS`

KORA NEWS is designed for African-first, globally interoperable news distribution. It separates editorial metadata and control logic from external publishing, media and distribution providers.

## Core capabilities

- News stories and updates
- Live breaking-news events
- Regions, countries and territories
- Topics and channels
- Editorial status and verification metadata
- Text, audio and video attachments
- Links to KORA TV and KORA RADIO live coverage
- Emergency/public-information priority
- Archive and correction history

## Safety and trust

Breaking and emergency information must carry explicit editorial state. KORA NEWS must not represent unverified material as confirmed fact. Emergency priority changes distribution priority; it does not bypass editorial verification requirements.

## Ownership

KORA owns the news metadata, topic graph, editorial workflow, distribution policy and user experience. External feeds, media storage, CDN, AI services and publishing endpoints are replaceable adapters.
