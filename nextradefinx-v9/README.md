# NexTradeFinX V9 — Multilingual Coach & Practice Journal

V9 turns the safe paper-trading workflow into a beginner-first learning experience.

## Adds
- language catalog with African-first launch coverage plus major global languages
- structured explainability contract: what happened, why, uncertainty, invalidation, risk lesson
- level-aware explanations for beginner, intermediate and experienced users
- behavioural risk coach for overtrading, revenge-trading patterns and oversizing
- pre-trade intention journal and post-trade reflection
- reward for disciplined abstention / NO TRADE decisions
- hard prohibition on personalized advice, profit promises and live execution

## Product rule
NexAI may explain a validated model output; it may not invent a probability, price target, return promise or trade instruction.

## Status
Practice and education only. No live broker connection. No client money. No personalized financial advice.
