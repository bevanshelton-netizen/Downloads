# NexTradeFinX V13 — Controlled Beta Product Policy

The first external beta is deliberately limited to education, NexLearn, NexAI explanations backed by validated model outputs, journaling, behavioural coaching and paper trading.

Conservative beta policy:
- invite-only
- verified email
- 18+ age confirmation for this financial-market beta
- current Terms, Privacy Notice and Risk Acknowledgement required
- no profit promises
- no live brokerage connection
- no client funds
- no leverage
- no personalized investment advice
- no public claim that internal Trader Readiness is a licence or qualification

The 18+ rule is a product-launch choice for the initial beta and should be reviewed with counsel before broader education access is designed.
