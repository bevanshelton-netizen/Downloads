# NexTradeFinX V10 — NexLearn Trader Readiness

V10 turns the beginner-first product into a structured learning pathway. It remains education and paper-trading only.

## What V10 adds

- learner profiles and progress events
- six competency stages from first principles to disciplined paper trading
- quiz scoring and mastery thresholds
- market-simulation completion tracking
- risk and behavioural discipline metrics
- readiness scoring with explicit blockers
- practice-only progression gate
- multilingual content keys compatible with the V9 language layer

## Readiness stages

0. Discover — money, markets and why prices move
1. Foundations — shares, forex, indices, commodities, crypto and order basics
2. Market Skills — charts, volatility, liquidity, spreads and time horizons
3. Risk First — position size, stop loss, drawdown, leverage and concentration
4. Analysis — evidence, probability, uncertainty and NexAI explanations
5. Practice Ready — disciplined paper trading with journaling and risk limits

## Important boundary

`NexTradeFinX Trader Readiness` is an internal educational competency status. It is not a statutory licence, professional qualification, financial-advice authorisation, broker approval or promise of trading success.

Real-money execution, client funds and personalised advice remain disabled.
