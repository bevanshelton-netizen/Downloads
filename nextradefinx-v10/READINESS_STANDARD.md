# NexTradeFinX Trader Readiness Standard

The readiness pathway is designed to measure educational progress and disciplined paper-trading behaviour before a learner can access advanced practice features.

## Evidence inputs

Readiness uses lesson completion, quiz mastery, completed simulations, journal completion, behavioural discipline, loss-limit adherence and position-sizing behaviour.

## Non-negotiable rules

- Passing readiness never enables real-money execution.
- Passing readiness never creates an investment-advice relationship.
- A learner with a daily-loss-limit breach cannot be marked practice ready until the relevant review policy is satisfied.
- Repeated oversized trade attempts block progression.
- A learner may be rewarded for disciplined abstention and NO TRADE decisions.
- The platform must display the educational/non-statutory nature of the status wherever the designation appears.

## Product principle

Progress is earned by understanding and discipline, not by simulated profit alone. A profitable paper trade made outside the risk plan must not improve readiness more than a disciplined loss or a justified NO TRADE decision.
