Community Guidelines

VIDEONOMY is being designed for creative freedom with clear safety boundaries. Do not upload content that unlawfully exploits children, facilitates serious wrongdoing, promotes terrorism, exposes private personal information, infringes copyright or other rights, contains non-consensual sexual material, or uses targeted threats and harassment to endanger people.

Spam, scams, impersonation, manipulated engagement and coordinated view inflation are also prohibited. Context matters for documentary, educational, artistic and newsworthy material, but context does not remove legal or safety responsibilities.

These founding-beta guidelines will become a more detailed moderation policy before broad public uploading is opened.
