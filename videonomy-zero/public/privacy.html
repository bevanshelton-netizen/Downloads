Founding Beta Privacy Notice

Version 2026-08-29. This is a launch-stage operational notice and should be reviewed by qualified counsel as the platform expands.

What we collect

When you apply as a creator, advertiser or partner, we collect the information you submit, such as your name, email address, phone number, message and selected launch offer. For creator accounts we also process creator profile details, session identifiers, video metadata and qualified-view records.

Why we use it

We use this information to assess applications, contact applicants, operate founding creator access, prevent abuse, administer videos, measure qualified viewing and manage launch partnerships.

Security and minimisation

Launch sessions are stored as cryptographic hashes where practical. Anti-abuse controls store salted hashes rather than raw IP addresses. We do not sell application data to advertisers.

Retention and rights

We retain records only as needed for the founding beta, legal/accounting obligations, fraud prevention and platform operations. South African users may request access, correction or deletion where applicable under POPIA. Contact details for formal privacy requests will be published with the production legal entity/domain email before general public launch.

International infrastructure

VIDEONOMY may use cloud infrastructure located outside South Africa. Where cross-border processing applies, we will use appropriate contractual and technical safeguards.

Questions during the founding beta can be submitted through the application/support forms on the main site.
