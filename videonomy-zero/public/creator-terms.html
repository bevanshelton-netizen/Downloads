Founding Creator Terms

Version 2026-08-29. Provisional founding-beta terms; formal legal review is required before scaled monetisation.

Your content

You must own or have sufficient rights to upload the content. You retain ownership of your content, while granting VIDEONOMY the permissions reasonably required to host, transmit, display, moderate, promote and technically process it for the service.

Founding beta

Initial creator access is invitation-only. The bootstrap upload format is MP4 with a 90 MB per-file limit. Features, limits and formats will change as the production video stack is funded.

Creator economics

The platform currently targets 70% of eligible watch-page ad revenue, 80% of the subscription viewing pool and 90% of eligible direct fan and brand-marketplace revenue for creators. These percentages do not guarantee earnings. Eligible revenue, external costs, taxes, fraud reversals and rights claims are accounted for before payout according to the applicable monetisation policy.

Integrity

Artificial views, self-dealing, bot traffic, payment fraud, rights abuse and attempts to manipulate creator earnings are prohibited and may result in withheld or reversed amounts and account suspension.

Moderation

Content may be limited or removed when required for safety, law, rights protection or platform integrity. Creators remain responsible for their uploads.
