Privacy

The platform is deliberately data-light.

PDEReady stores ordinary learner progress on the user's own device. The learner name, diagnostic attempts, case-practice scores, eligibility checklist and readiness history are stored in browser local storage. They are not sent to a PDEReady account server in this version.

What the current PDEReady page stores

When a user chooses to save a name or completes preparation activity, the browser may store that information locally so progress remains available on the same device and browser.

Payments through iKhokha

Premium checkout is processed by iKhokha. PDEReady sends the selected product, amount and a unique transaction reference to the iKhokha payment service. Payment-card details are entered on and processed by iKhokha's secure payment environment; this site does not store card numbers or CVV values.

After payment, the platform may verify the transaction status with iKhokha using the unique payment reference and place a secure access cookie on the customer's device so paid access can be recognised for the relevant access period.

How to remove local progress

Users can clear the site's browser storage/cookies through their browser settings. Clearing site data will remove locally saved PDEReady progress and may also remove the device's premium-access cookie, in which case payment verification or support may be required to restore access.

External links

The platform links to official regulator and professional-body websites and may link to standalone preparation products. Those services operate under their own privacy policies.

Future accounts

If authenticated cloud learner accounts or expanded analytics are activated later, this notice must be updated before those features are treated as part of the public service.
