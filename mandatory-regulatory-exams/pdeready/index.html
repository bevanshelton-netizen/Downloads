CURRENT EXAM ROUTE

Know which pathway you are actually on.

PPRA changed the entry architecture in 2026. PDEReady separates the existing qualification route from the newer alternative competency-based route so candidates do not study from outdated assumptions.

PDE4 EXISTING ROUTE

Non-principal pathway

Use the official PPRA portal eligibility rules for your status. The current requirements include approved practical-training/qualification information on the PPRA system and other eligibility controls.

NEW FROM 1 AUG 2026

Alternative PPRA Examination

PPRA introduced an additional competency-based route with Matric as the minimum educational entry requirement. The examination uses the same content as PDE4, followed by six prescribed practical training modules. This route does not confer the PPRE professional designation.

PDE5

Principal progression

PDE5 remains the advanced route associated with principal-level professional competence. Confirm your current NQF Level 5 / exemption and Full Status FFC eligibility directly with PPRA before enrolment.

Next ordinary sitting: 12 November 2026. The current PPRA calendar page lists MyPPRA registration from 1 October to 31 October 2026. Always confirm the live MyPPRA/PPRA notice before paying because dates may be amended or extended.
