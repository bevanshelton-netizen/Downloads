# PDEReady — PDE4 & PDE5 preparation

PDEReady is the Property Practice pathway inside the Mandatory Regulatory Exams Platform.

## Current 2026 product scope
- 40 original topic-tagged preparation questions (20 PDE4 + 20 PDE5)
- 12 original case-response scenarios
- persistent on-device learner profile and attempt history
- adaptive 10-question diagnostics
- weak-area study plan and focused topic drills
- case-response structure scoring
- internal readiness indicator and exam countdown
- PDE4 diagnostic and study map
- PDE5 diagnostic and principal-level study map
- eligibility pre-flight checklist
- 2026/2027 PPRA exam-calendar awareness
- alternative competency-based route awareness from 1 August 2026
- original scenario-based practice
- case-response writing coach
- five-hour take-home mock timer
- exam-integrity boundary

## Current official position reflected in the build
The PPRA describes the PDE as a take-home examination. For the 2026 sittings, the Authority instructed candidates to download the paper, answer all questions in the supplied document and submit within the announced window.

The published 2026/2027 calendar lists:
- 12 November 2026 exam
- registration opens 1 October 2026
- registration/payment closes 31 October 2026
- 11 February 2027 exam
- registration opens 4 January 2027
- registration/payment closes 29 January 2027

In July 2026 the PPRA announced an additional competency-based pathway effective 1 August 2026. The alternative examination uses the same content as PDE4, accepts a National Senior Certificate (Matric) as the stated minimum educational entry requirement, and retains six prescribed practical training modules. Candidates on that alternative route do not obtain the PPRE designation merely by using that pathway.

## Content policy
PDEReady is independent preparation material. It must not:
- claim affiliation with or endorsement by the PPRA;
- copy confidential or unlawfully obtained examination material;
- generate answers for a live sitting;
- imply that an internal readiness score is an official PPRA result;
- replace a candidate's obligation to confirm current eligibility, fees, dates and rules with PPRA.

PPRA's 2026 take-home instructions state that AI tools such as ChatGPT are prohibited during the live examination. PDEReady is therefore deliberately a **pre-exam learning and mock-practice product**, not a live-exam answering service.
