Refund & Cancellation Policy

Applies to paid digital PDEReady preparation packages sold by IZAKHONO AFRICA (PTY) LTD.

Merchant: IZAKHONO AFRICA (PTY) LTD • Registration 2022/699539/07
Payment provider: iKhokha

1. Before access is activated

If payment is cancelled or fails before successful verification, paid access is not activated. No charge should be treated as complete unless the transaction is confirmed by the payment provider.

2. Duplicate or erroneous payments

If the same customer is charged twice for the same intended package, or an incorrect amount is charged due to a platform error, contact us promptly with the transaction reference. Once verified, the duplicate or erroneous charge will be corrected or refunded as appropriate.

3. Digital access after activation

Because PDEReady Premium is digital content made available immediately after successful payment, customers should review the package description before paying. A refund request made after access is activated will be considered where the paid service was materially unavailable, materially misdescribed, duplicated, or where applicable law requires a remedy.

4. Change of mind

Where the service has already been activated and is functioning as described, a change-of-mind request may not automatically qualify for a refund. Any statutory consumer rights that apply remain unaffected.

5. Service failure

If paid access cannot be delivered because of a platform fault that we cannot restore within a reasonable period, we may extend the access period, provide an equivalent remedy, or refund the affected payment depending on the circumstances.

6. How to request help or a refund

Email info@izakhonoafrica.co.za with the purchaser name, payment date, package and iKhokha transaction/payment reference. Do not send card numbers or CVV information.

7. Processing

Approved refunds are processed back through the available payment channel or another lawful method agreed with the customer. Bank and payment-provider processing times may apply after the refund is issued.

Last updated: 17 September 2026. This policy does not limit rights that cannot lawfully be excluded under South African consumer law.
