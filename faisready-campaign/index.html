All South African RE Preparation Courses

RE1 Exam Preparation

For: FSPs and Key Individuals in all categories of FSPs, including applicable Compliance Officers.

Official exam: 80 questions.

Prepare for the regulatory duties, legislation, governance, fit-and-proper obligations and compliance knowledge assessed in RE1.

RE3 Exam Preparation

For: FSPs and Key Individuals in Categories II and IIA.

Official exam: 30 questions. RE3 is written in addition to RE1 where applicable.

Prepare for the Code of Conduct and regulatory requirements relevant to Categories II and IIA.

RE4 Exam Preparation

For: FSPs and Key Individuals in Category III.

Official exam: 30 questions. RE4 is written in addition to RE1 where applicable.

Prepare for the Code of Conduct and regulatory obligations applicable to Category III administrative FSPs.

RE5 Exam Preparation

For: Representatives in all categories of FSPs, subject to regulatory exceptions.

Official exam: 50 questions.

Prepare for representative duties, disclosure, conduct, competence and regulatory responsibilities tested in RE5.
