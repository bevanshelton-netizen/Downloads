PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy at FAISReady

FAISReady is operated by IZAKHONO AFRICA (PTY) LTD. We aim to collect only the information reasonably required to provide the service, support customers, reconcile payments and protect the platform.

1. Information we may collect

This may include information a customer or learner submits, such as name, email address, phone number, company details, selected product, support correspondence, consent records, learner progress where applicable, and transaction references or payment status.

2. Payments

Payment processing may be handled by an external payment provider. FAISReady may receive transaction identifiers, status, amount and limited payment metadata needed for reconciliation and support. We do not intentionally collect full card numbers through the public FAISReady pages.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to create or activate access, provide learner services, respond to support requests, reconcile payments, prevent fraud or abuse, maintain required business records, improve service reliability, and send communications that are necessary for the service or that the user has agreed to receive.

4. No behavioural advertising profile

FAISReady does not use the public product experience to build behavioural advertising profiles or advertising identifiers. Commercial referral information may be used only where needed to attribute an explicit referral or transaction.

5. Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers that are necessary to operate the platform, process payments, provide infrastructure, deliver communications or meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information to advertisers.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect customer information. No online system can be guaranteed completely risk-free, so suspected security issues should be reported promptly.

7. Retention

Information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the service, support, transaction reconciliation, legal/compliance obligations and legitimate record-keeping requirements, after which it may be deleted or de-identified where appropriate.

8. Access and correction

A customer may contact us to request access to or correction of personal information, or to raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify identity before disclosing or changing account information.

9. Contact

Privacy and customer-support requests can be submitted through the FAISReady support contact displayed on the main website.

See also the Terms, Refunds, Cancellation & Digital Delivery Policy.
