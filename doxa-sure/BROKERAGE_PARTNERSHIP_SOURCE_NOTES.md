# DOXA-SURE Brokerage Partnership Pack — Source Notes

The brokerage presentation pack should be reviewed against current South African regulatory sources before a live regulated pilot. Core source families include:

- Financial Advisory and Intermediary Services Act (FAIS)
- General Code of Conduct for Authorised Financial Services Providers and Representatives
- FSCA FAIS e-services / representative-register processes
- Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and Information Regulator guidance
- Applicable insurance/product conduct requirements
- Applicable National Credit Act/NCR rules where debt counselling or credit-related interventions are involved

Operational principle: DOXA-SURE remains a marketplace/technology layer unless and until a separate lawful representative structure has been formally created. A commercial MOU alone does not create regulatory authority.

Current FSCA e-services provide functions to query FSP details and maintain/request representative registers. This should be part of the onboarding verification workflow.

This file is a compliance-research reminder and not a legal opinion.
