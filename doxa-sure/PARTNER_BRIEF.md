# DOXA-SURE Rescue Network — Founding Partner Brief

## What DOXA-SURE is building
DOXA-SURE is a bootstrap-first asset-protection and rescue-readiness platform. The first version helps consumers act earlier when a home, vehicle, income stream or business asset is under financial pressure.

DOXA-SURE itself does not perform regulated legal, debt-counselling or financial-product services unless and until appropriately authorised. Cases requiring those services are prepared and referred to verified professionals.

## Who we want in the founding network
- Attorneys experienced in credit enforcement, foreclosure, repossession and consumer matters
- NCR-registered debt counsellors
- Authorised FSPs / insurance specialists
- Credit-life / claims specialists operating within an authorised structure
- Property and vehicle specialists for least-destructive exit strategies when retention is no longer realistic

## What the platform gives a professional
A referred case should arrive with a clearer file:
- Customer identity/contact details collected only to the extent required and lawfully permitted
- Asset summary
- Arrears position supplied by the customer
- Relevant dated documents
- Timeline of events and communications
- Rescue Case number
- Reason for referral
- Urgency classification

This is intended to reduce wasted first-contact time and help the professional identify the correct regulated pathway faster.

## Founding-pilot commercial principle
No prohibited or disguised referral-fee arrangements. Any remuneration must be documented, lawful for the professional category and reviewed before activation. During early validation, the preferred approach is simple reciprocal service collaboration rather than complex commission structures.

## Service standard
Founding partners should commit to:
1. Registration/compliance verification.
2. Clear scope and fee disclosure directly to the customer.
3. No guarantees of outcomes.
4. Prompt acknowledgement of critical referrals.
5. Basic status feedback to DOXA-SURE where the customer has consented.
6. Protection of personal information and confidential documents.

## Pilot proposition
DOXA-SURE supplies organised, triaged rescue-readiness cases. The partner supplies the regulated expertise that DOXA-SURE must not pretend to provide.
