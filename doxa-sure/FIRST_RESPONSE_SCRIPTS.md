# DOXA-SURE First Human Response Scripts

Use these during the founding pilot so every prospect gets a calm, consistent, human response. Do not diagnose legal rights, promise outcomes, interpret policies, or present DOXA-SURE as an insurer.

## General first reply
Hello. Thank you for contacting DOXA-SURE. I’m sorry you are dealing with this pressure. We will start by understanding what is at risk, what stage the matter has reached, and what documents you already have. We do not need your banking password, PIN or online-banking login details.

Please tell us only:
1. What asset are you worried about — home, vehicle, income, business asset or other?
2. Are payments current, one payment behind, two or more behind, or are you unsure?
3. Have you received any formal letter, Section 129 notice, summons, court paper, repossession notice or auction/sale notice?
4. Has your income recently reduced or stopped?
5. Do you know whether credit-life or payment-protection cover exists?

## Green result
Your current answers place you in the Green early-warning category. That does not mean nothing can change; it means this is a good time to prepare before a crisis. The best next step is to keep the key finance documents together, confirm payment dates and identify whether any credit-life/payment-protection cover exists.

## Amber result
Your current answers place you in the Amber category. This is the stage where early action matters. Please avoid waiting for another missed payment before organising your documents and communication history. We can help you prepare the Rescue Readiness file if you want the next layer of support.

## Red result
Your current answers place you in the Red category. Please treat the matter as urgent. Gather the latest statement, any notices or letters, proof of the income change and a simple timeline of communication with the lender/provider. DOXA-SURE can organise the factual Rescue Readiness file and prepare a referral file where authorised professional help is required.

## Critical result
Your answers indicate a Critical situation because formal legal or enforcement activity may already be underway. Legal deadlines can continue to run. Please do not delay seeking suitably qualified professional assistance while waiting for a DOXA-SURE pilot service. We can still help organise the factual file, but we cannot represent that a legal process has been stopped.

## R199 Rescue Readiness Pack response
The founding-pilot Rescue Readiness Pack is R199 once-off. It is a human-assisted case-organisation service. We organise the factual asset position, document checklist, communication timeline, key missing information and referral preparation where regulated help is required. It is not insurance cover, legal representation, debt counselling or a promise of a particular lender outcome.

Before we proceed, we will confirm what information is actually necessary and avoid collecting unnecessary sensitive details.

## R99 Shield response
The founding DOXA-SURE Shield is R99 per month. During the pilot it is a rescue-readiness membership focused on keeping the member’s asset-risk picture, document readiness and Rescue Case history organised as the platform is activated. It is not an insurance policy or a substitute for regulated professional services.

## End every conversation with a clear next step
Use one of these:
- “Your next step is to send only the documents we listed.”
- “Your next step is to contact the qualified professional we identified.”
- “Your next step is to run the free Asset Risk Check and send us the result.”
- “Your next step is the R199 Rescue Readiness Pack if you want us to organise the case file.”
- “Your next step is simply to keep monitoring the position for now.”

## Never say
- We will stop the bank.
- Your home or car is safe now.
- We can cancel the debt.
- Ignore the lender or court documents.
- Credit-life will definitely pay.
- We are your insurer.
- We provide debt counselling or legal representation unless that service is actually being performed by the appropriately authorised partner under the approved model.
