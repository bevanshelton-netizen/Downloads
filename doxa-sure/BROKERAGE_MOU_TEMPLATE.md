# DOXA-SURE / Participating Brokerage
# Draft Memorandum of Understanding (MOU)

**Discussion draft — not binding except where expressly stated.**

This Memorandum of Understanding is entered into between:

**DOXA-SURE / [DOXA-SURE operating entity]** ("DOXA-SURE")

and

**[Brokerage legal name]**, FSP No. **[____]** ("Brokerage").

## 1. Purpose

The parties intend to explore and, once all required compliance steps are complete, operate a customer-matching and technology relationship in which DOXA-SURE provides non-regulated marketplace, intake, workflow and technology services and the Brokerage provides regulated financial services within the scope of its authorisation.

## 2. No licence lending or transfer

Nothing in this MOU transfers, rents, lends, sublicenses or otherwise makes the Brokerage's FSP licence available to DOXA-SURE as though it were DOXA-SURE's own licence.

DOXA-SURE must not describe itself as an authorised FSP unless and until it independently holds the relevant authorisation.

If the parties later agree that DOXA-SURE or a related entity will operate as a representative or juristic representative of the Brokerage, that relationship must be created by separate formal documentation and completed regulatory/compliance processes before any regulated activity is performed under that structure.

## 3. Roles

### 3.1 DOXA-SURE
DOXA-SURE may:
- market the marketplace and technology service;
- capture broad customer needs and non-advisory intake information;
- route opportunities according to documented eligibility and service criteria;
- provide workflow, reminder, readiness and customer-experience technology;
- maintain marketplace audit records;
- provide approved white-label technology where separately agreed.

DOXA-SURE must not, unless separately and lawfully authorised:
- recommend a specific financial product as suitable for a customer;
- perform regulated intermediary services;
- hold itself out as the Brokerage or as the holder of the Brokerage's FSP licence;
- perform debt counselling, legal representation or binder functions merely because this MOU exists.

### 3.2 Brokerage
The Brokerage will:
- provide accurate FSP, category/subcategory and representative information;
- notify DOXA-SURE of material changes to authorisation or service capability;
- perform regulated financial services through appropriately authorised and supervised persons;
- provide required disclosures and records;
- manage advice, quotations, policy placement and other regulated activity within its lawful authority;
- handle relevant regulated-service complaints;
- participate in compliance review of customer-facing regulated wording.

## 4. Verification and activation

The Brokerage is not activated as a verified marketplace participant until DOXA-SURE has completed the agreed onboarding checks and the Brokerage has confirmed the accuracy of its service profile.

DOXA-SURE may suspend matching if authorisation, service scope, complaints, capacity or compliance status becomes uncertain.

## 5. Matching principles

Customer matching will be based on documented factors that may include:
- customer need;
- verified authorisation/service scope;
- geography;
- customer type;
- insurer/product access declared by the Brokerage;
- service capacity;
- response performance;
- other objectively documented fit criteria.

A higher subscription or lead fee must not make a brokerage eligible for a customer request it is not authorised or operationally suited to handle.

## 6. Customer identity and disclosures

Before regulated activity begins, the customer must be able to identify the Brokerage/FSP responsible for the regulated service and receive the disclosures required by the Brokerage's compliant process.

## 7. Fees

The commercial model selected by the parties will be recorded in a schedule.

Suggested launch options:
- Founding 50: R0 joining fee; R0 monthly fee for first 3 months; R50 per accepted qualified lead;
- Network: R499/month + R75 per accepted qualified lead;
- Broker Pro: R1,499/month + R40 per accepted qualified lead;
- White Label: R4,950/month + R25 per accepted qualified lead, with implementation quoted separately;
- Enterprise: from R12,500/month, scope negotiated.

Any remuneration, financial interest, commission or fee connected to regulated financial services must be reviewed for compliance before implementation.

## 8. Lead acceptance

A lead fee becomes payable only where the Brokerage affirmatively accepts an opportunity in accordance with the agreed system/process. Duplicate, test, obviously invalid or out-of-scope leads should not be charged unless otherwise agreed in writing.

## 9. Data protection

Each party must comply with applicable privacy and data-protection obligations. Before production data sharing begins, the parties will agree:
- responsible-party/operator roles where applicable;
- permitted purposes;
- minimum data fields;
- security controls;
- incident notification;
- retention/deletion;
- data-subject requests;
- approved subprocessors and communication channels where necessary.

## 10. Confidentiality

Each party will keep confidential non-public business, customer, security, pricing and technical information received from the other, subject to legal/regulatory disclosure requirements.

## 11. Marketing and branding

Neither party may use the other's regulatory status, trademarks, insurer relationships or product claims in a misleading manner.

Any customer-facing statement that could reasonably be understood as regulated product marketing or advice-related disclosure should follow the Brokerage's compliance approval process before publication.

## 12. Complaints

The parties will implement a complaints-routing matrix distinguishing:
- DOXA-SURE technology/marketplace complaints;
- Brokerage advice/intermediary-service complaints;
- insurer/product/claim complaints;
- privacy complaints;
- legal/debt-counselling referrals outside the FSP relationship.

## 13. Pilot

The initial pilot will be limited to agreed categories, geography/capacity and customer volumes. The parties will review match quality, response times, complaints, conversion, customer feedback and compliance exceptions before expanding.

## 14. Term and termination

The initial MOU term is [90 days / 6 months] unless extended in writing. Either party may terminate the pilot on [30] days' written notice, subject to orderly completion/transfer of active customer matters and legal record-retention requirements.

Immediate suspension or termination may occur for material regulatory, customer-harm, data-security, misrepresentation or fraud concerns.

## 15. Non-exclusivity

Unless a separate written schedule states otherwise, the relationship is non-exclusive. DOXA-SURE may onboard multiple eligible brokerages and the Brokerage may use other distribution/technology channels.

## 16. No guarantee

DOXA-SURE does not guarantee customer volume, policy placement, conversion, premium, claim outcome or revenue. The Brokerage does not guarantee that every referred customer can be offered cover.

## 17. Governing law

This MOU and any resulting agreements will be interpreted under the laws of the Republic of South Africa, subject to any mandatory financial-sector or consumer-protection requirements.

## 18. Binding status

Unless the parties expressly mark a clause as binding, this MOU records the intended framework only. Confidentiality, data-protection duties, permitted brand use and any agreed payment obligations incurred during an authorised pilot may be made binding by signature or a separate schedule.

## 19. Conditions before regulated go-live

No regulated insurance activity will be launched through the DOXA-SURE/Brokerage relationship until the parties have completed the compliance steps required for the selected operating model, which may include:
- FSP status/category verification;
- approved customer disclosures;
- representative/mandate documentation where applicable;
- data-sharing agreement;
- complaints allocation;
- remuneration review;
- approved scripts/marketing wording;
- record-keeping process;
- security/POPIA checks.
