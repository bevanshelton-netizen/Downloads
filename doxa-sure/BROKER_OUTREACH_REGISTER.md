# DOXA-SURE Broker Outreach Register

This register supports the DOXA-SURE Broker Network acquisition campaign.

## Purpose
Track publicly identified South African insurance brokerages that may be suitable for the DOXA-SURE Founding Broker Network, Broker Pro, White Label or Enterprise partnership models.

## Non-negotiable compliance rule
A prospect being listed here does **not** mean DOXA-SURE has verified, appointed, endorsed or partnered with that brokerage. Before any customer-facing match or regulated workflow is activated, the brokerage's current FSCA/FSP status, authorised categories/subcategories, key individual/compliance structure and agreed operating scope must be checked and recorded.

DOXA-SURE does not borrow, rent or present another firm's FSP licence as its own. An MOU does not itself create representative authority.

## Priority logic
- **A** — strongest strategic fit: digital, national/multi-region, platform-minded, embedded/white-label, strong personal + commercial range or unusually aligned customer-service model.
- **B** — strong brokerage prospect: credible independent/established broker with useful product/geographic coverage.
- **C** — expansion/strategic prospect: useful regional density, specialist capability, underwriting/enterprise angle, or requires extra verification before outreach.

## Outreach sequence
1. Confirm public contact route and current FSP status.
2. Send concise partnership introduction with the live partner pack.
3. Ask for a 45-minute discussion with principal/owner plus Key Individual/compliance representative where appropriate.
4. Record response and meeting date.
5. Before onboarding, complete the DOXA-SURE brokerage go-live checklist.
6. No customer routing until status is `verified_for_matching` and the permitted service scope is recorded.
