## Cold-call opener
Good day. I am calling from DOXA-SURE. We are piloting a new asset-protection support service for people who are worried about falling behind on a bond, vehicle or other important commitment. We do not promise to cancel debt or stop lawful enforcement. Our job is to help people act earlier, organise the facts and prepare the correct rescue pathway before matters get worse. May I ask you three quick questions to see whether the free Asset Risk Check is relevant?

## Three qualification questions
1. Are you currently worried about keeping your home, vehicle, business asset or income position?
2. Are payments still current, already behind, or have you received formal correspondence?
3. Do you know whether you have credit-life or another policy linked to the debt?

## Language we must never use
- We will stop the bank.
- We guarantee your house/car will be saved.
- We can cancel your debt.
- Join today and we will pay your arrears.
- We are your insurer. (until that is legally true)
