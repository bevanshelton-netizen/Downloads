# DOXA-SURE First 100 Customers Playbook

## Objective
Prove that people will use and pay for DOXA-SURE before spending money on insurance infrastructure, paid AI, SMS, OCR, dedicated hosting or a large team.

## Founding offer
- Free Asset Risk Check — R0
- Rescue Readiness Pack — R199 once-off
- DOXA-SURE Shield — R99/month

These are bootstrap assistance services only. Do not market them as insurance, legal representation, debt counselling, financial-product advice or a guarantee that an asset will be saved.

## First-customer target
The first target is not 10,000 users. It is:
- 100 completed risk checks
- 25 paid Rescue Readiness Packs
- 10 Shield members
- at least 10 customer interviews explaining why they joined or did not join

This would produce R4,975 once-off pack revenue plus R990 monthly recurring Shield revenue, before any additional upsells.

## Zero-cost acquisition channels
1. WhatsApp status and direct messages to people who already know the founder.
2. Community Facebook groups where promotion is permitted.
3. Homeowner, vehicle-owner, SME and retrenchment-support groups.
4. Churches and community organisations willing to share the free risk check.
5. Estate agents and property practitioners who encounter distressed sellers.
6. Used-car dealers and vehicle-finance networks that encounter payment pressure.
7. Accountants/bookkeepers serving small businesses.
8. Attorneys, registered debt counsellors and authorised FSPs willing to receive correctly triaged referrals.
9. Short educational videos explaining what to do before arrears become enforcement.
10. Referral from every satisfied pilot customer.

## Public message
Lead with the problem, not the product:

**Worried about losing your home, vehicle or another asset because money has become tight? DOXA-SURE gives you a free 60-second Asset Risk Check so you can see how urgent the situation is and organise the facts before the problem gets worse.**

Never lead with "we stop banks" or "we save your house".

## Daily founder rhythm — first 14 days
- 20 direct outreach conversations
- 5 follow-ups from previous day
- 5 free risk checks completed
- 2 Rescue Pack offers made only after the free check
- 1 partner conversation
- Record only aggregate numbers in the repository; never put customer PII in GitHub

## Conversion path
1. Prospect sees education/post/direct message.
2. Prospect completes Free Asset Risk Check.
3. If Green: offer Shield only if they want ongoing readiness.
4. If Amber: offer Shield and explain early-action value.
5. If Red: offer Rescue Readiness Pack for human-assisted case organisation.
6. If Critical: clearly warn that qualified professional help may be urgent; do not delay referral merely to sell a pack.
7. After delivery, ask permission for anonymous feedback/testimonial.

## What to learn before spending money
We need answers to these questions:
- Which asset creates the most demand: home, vehicle, income or business?
- At what stage do people ask for help?
- Will people pay R199 for organised rescue-readiness assistance?
- Will they retain a R99/month Shield membership?
- Which referrals are most commonly required?
- Which documents are most often missing?
- Which acquisition channel converts without paid advertising?

## Spend gate
Do not add a paid tool merely because it would be convenient. Add it only when one of these is true:
1. manual work is preventing paid sales;
2. customer safety/compliance requires it;
3. the tool clearly saves more money than it costs; or
4. recurring revenue already covers it comfortably.

## First revenue allocation
1. Professional legal/compliance review of the public offer.
2. Dedicated DOXA-SURE database/hosting only when the shared bootstrap becomes a real constraint.
3. Secure operational communications/document handling.
4. Payment automation.
5. Professional network onboarding.
6. Underwriting/cell-captive development only after customer demand is proven.
