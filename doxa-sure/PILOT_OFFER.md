# DOXA-SURE Bootstrap Pilot Offer

## Purpose
Generate first revenue before paying for insurer infrastructure, paid AI, SMS, OCR, a separate Supabase project or a large team.

## Customer promise
**Protect what you worked for before it is too late.**

DOXA-SURE helps a customer organise their asset-risk position early, build a dated Rescue file, identify missing documents and prepare the case for the appropriate lender, insurer or authorised professional process.

DOXA-SURE does **not** promise that an asset can be saved and the bootstrap pilot does not sell insurance, legal representation or debt counselling.

## Simple launch funnel

### 1. Free Asset Risk Check — R0
A short entry assessment designed to answer:
- What is at risk?
- Is the account current, one payment behind or already in serious arrears?
- Has a Section 129/legal notice arrived?
- Is credit-life cover known or unknown?
- What information is missing?

The outcome is Green / Amber / Red / Critical and a next-step category. No regulated recommendation is provided by the automated check.

### 2. Rescue Readiness Pack — R199 once-off founding-pilot price
A human-assisted administration and preparation service:
- Asset-risk summary
- Dated Rescue Case record
- Document checklist
- Credit-life document check list (identification only, not policy advice)
- Timeline of lender/provider communications supplied by the customer
- Priority action checklist
- Referral preparation where an attorney, registered debt counsellor, authorised FSP or insurer is required

**Not included:** legal advice, debt counselling, financial product advice, insurance benefits or guaranteed lender concessions.

### 3. DOXA-SURE Shield — R99/month founding-pilot membership
Ongoing non-insurance rescue-readiness membership:
- Shield dashboard
- Asset Vault tracking
- Shield Score
- Reminder schedule
- Rescue Case history
- Document checklist and private vault when live storage is enabled
- Escalation-readiness record

Cancel anytime during the pilot.

## First cash targets
- 25 Rescue Readiness Packs = R4,975
- 50 Rescue Readiness Packs = R9,950
- 100 Shield members = R9,900 monthly recurring revenue
- 250 Shield members = R24,750 monthly recurring revenue

## What first revenue should pay for
1. Business/legal/compliance review of the public offer.
2. Dedicated DOXA-SURE hosting/database when needed.
3. Secure communications and document operations.
4. Payment automation.
5. Professional partner onboarding.
6. Only then: underwriting/cell-captive product development.

## Non-negotiable sales rule
Never market the bootstrap pilot as insurance, never promise a bank will stop enforcement, and never imply that buying membership changes an existing credit agreement or legal deadline.
