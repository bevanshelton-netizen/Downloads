What DOXA-SURE is — and is not — selling today

These founding-pilot services organise information, identify urgency and prepare rescue-readiness records. They are not an insurance policy, legal representation, debt counselling, financial-product advice, a promise that a bank will stop enforcement, or a guarantee that an asset can be saved.

If summons, court papers, repossession documents or a sale-in-execution process has started, qualified professional help may be urgent and legal time periods continue to run.
