Non-negotiable controls

Built to protect the customer and the FSP.

✓ FSP verificationNo “verified” marketplace status before status and service scope are checked.
✓ Paid plan cannot create eligibilityA higher fee cannot buy access to an out-of-scope customer request.
✓ Customer sees the responsible FSPThe regulated journey cannot hide who is providing the financial service.
✓ POPIA/data-sharing gateProduction customer transfer starts only after the privacy/security model is agreed.
✓ No borrowed-licence claimsDOXA-SURE does not become an FSP because a brokerage signs an MOU.
✓ Separate representative processA juristic-representative model, if selected, requires separate formal compliance and representative-register steps.

Regulatory reference points

The production operating model should be checked against the Financial Advisory and Intermediary Services Act framework, the General Code of Conduct for authorised FSPs and representatives, FSCA representative-register requirements, POPIA/data-protection requirements and any product-specific insurance rules that apply. Debt counselling and legal representation remain separate regulatory/professional frameworks.

This pack is a commercial discussion document, not legal advice or regulatory approval.
