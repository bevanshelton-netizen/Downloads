Privacy & POPIA Notice

Founding-pilot privacy notice.

1. Purpose

DOXA-SURE aims to collect only information reasonably needed to run the Free Asset Risk Check, provide on-site Help Desk responses, organise a Rescue Readiness Pack, maintain a Shield pilot record or prepare an appropriate referral.

2. Information we may handle

This may include the type of asset at risk, payment status, income-change information, documents a customer later chooses to provide through an approved private process, and a chronology of communications. Customers must not provide banking passwords, PINs, security answers or online-banking login credentials.

3. Public website

The public Free Asset Risk Check and automated Help Desk are designed not to require an ID number, bank account number or banking credentials. The public site does not upload customer documents.

4. Data minimisation

Customers are encouraged to share only the minimum information needed. Sensitive customer documents must not be placed in public GitHub repositories or public links.

5. Automated Help Desk

The current public Help Desk is an automated pilot assistant that responds on the website. Browser speech features may read replies aloud. It is not represented as a substitute for regulated professional advice.

6. Sharing

DOXA-SURE should not sell personal information. Information should be shared with a professional or service provider only where reasonably necessary for the requested service, with appropriate authority or where law requires it.

7. Security and retention

Reasonable organisational and technical safeguards should be used. Personal information should be kept only as long as reasonably necessary for the service, legal/accounting obligations, dispute handling or a customer-authorised continuing relationship.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, a customer may ask what personal information is held, request correction, object to certain processing or ask for deletion where retention is no longer justified.

9. Privacy requests

Privacy questions can be initiated through the on-site DOXA-SURE Help Desk. This interim notice should receive professional POPIA/compliance review as the pilot scales.
