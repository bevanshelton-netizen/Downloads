Privacy & POPIA Notice

Founding-pilot privacy notice. Version 1 — 25 August 2026.

1. Purpose

DOXA-SURE aims to collect only information reasonably needed to run the Free Asset Risk Check, respond through WhatsApp, organise a Rescue Readiness Pack, maintain a Shield pilot record, or prepare an appropriate referral.

2. Information we may handle

This may include contact details, the type of asset at risk, lender/provider name, payment status, income-change information, documents the customer chooses to provide, and a chronology of communications. Customers should not send banking passwords, PINs, security answers or online-banking login credentials.

3. Why we use information

Information is used to respond to the customer, assess urgency, organise the factual case, prepare requested pilot services, maintain service records, and facilitate an authorised-professional referral when needed.

4. Sharing

DOXA-SURE should not sell personal information. Information should be shared with a professional or service provider only where reasonably necessary for the requested service, where the customer has authorised the referral or where law requires it.

5. Data minimisation

The public Free Asset Risk Check is designed not to require an ID number, bank account number or banking credentials. Customers are encouraged to share the minimum information necessary for the task.

6. Security

DOXA-SURE should use reasonable organisational and technical safeguards. Sensitive customer documents must not be placed in public GitHub repositories or public links. The current public landing page does not itself upload customer documents.

7. Retention

Personal information should be kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the pilot service, legal/accounting requirements, dispute handling or a customer-authorised continuing Shield relationship, after which it should be securely deleted or de-identified where appropriate.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, a customer may ask what personal information DOXA-SURE holds, request correction of inaccurate information, object to certain processing, or ask for deletion where retention is no longer justified.

9. WhatsApp and third parties

When customers use WhatsApp or another third-party service, that provider also processes information under its own terms and privacy practices. Customers should avoid sending unnecessary sensitive data through messaging channels.

10. Contact

Privacy requests can be initiated through the DOXA-SURE WhatsApp Business Assistant at 066 298 2213. This interim notice should receive professional POPIA/compliance review as the pilot scales.
