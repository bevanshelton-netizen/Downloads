How matching works

Not the highest bidder. The best lawful fit.

The matching logic is designed around customer need and brokerage eligibility—not who pays the biggest fee.

01

Understand the need

Insurance quote, claim help, home, vehicle, business, life, income protection or another supported request.

02

Check authority

Only brokerages whose verified authorisation and declared scope fit the request remain eligible.

03

Check fit

Location, insurer/product access, customer type, service capacity and response-time preferences.

04

Identify the FSP

The customer is shown which brokerage/FSP is responsible for the regulated service.

Customer matching

Tell us what you need.

This founding-network matcher does not give insurance advice. It prepares a category request so a verified participating FSP can assist once the network has suitable broker coverage.

Do not enter ID numbers, bank account details, policy passwords, PINs or online-banking credentials here.
