Refund & Cancellation Policy

Founding-pilot policy.

1. Free Asset Risk Check

The Free Asset Risk Check costs R0 and therefore has no payment to refund.

2. Rescue Readiness Pack — R199 once-off

If a customer cancels before DOXA-SURE has materially started the personalised pack, the customer may request a full refund. If substantial work has already been completed or the pack has been delivered, refund eligibility will depend on the circumstances and applicable consumer law.

3. DOXA-SURE Shield — R99/month

A customer may cancel the founding Shield membership at any time. Cancellation stops future monthly charges after the current paid period, subject to applicable law and the payment arrangement actually in use.

4. Payment status

Live PayFast checkout is not represented as active until the existing merchant account completes live verification. No automatic recurring payment is represented as active unless it has actually been configured and authorised.

5. Service failure

If DOXA-SURE accepts payment but cannot provide the agreed pilot service, an appropriate remedy or refund should be offered in accordance with applicable consumer law.

6. Urgent legal matters

A customer should not buy a pilot service as a substitute for urgent legal or regulated professional assistance. If the matter is clearly outside the pilot scope, appropriate escalation should take priority.

7. How to ask about cancellation or refunds

Use the on-site DOXA-SURE Help Desk on the main website for general refund and cancellation information. Do not provide banking passwords, PINs or login credentials.
