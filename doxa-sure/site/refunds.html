Refund & Cancellation Policy

Founding-pilot policy. Version 1 — 25 August 2026.

1. Free Asset Risk Check

The Free Asset Risk Check costs R0 and therefore has no payment to refund.

2. Rescue Readiness Pack — R199 once-off

If a customer cancels before DOXA-SURE has materially started the personalised pack, the customer may request a full refund. If substantial work has already been completed or the pack has been delivered, refund eligibility will depend on the circumstances and applicable consumer law. DOXA-SURE should not charge for work that was not agreed or not delivered.

3. DOXA-SURE Shield — R99/month

A customer may cancel the founding Shield membership at any time. Cancellation stops future monthly charges after the current paid period, unless applicable law or an agreed payment arrangement requires a different outcome. No automatic debit or recurring payment should be represented as active unless it has actually been configured and authorised.

4. Service failure

If DOXA-SURE accepts payment but cannot provide the agreed pilot service, a refund or other appropriate remedy should be offered in accordance with applicable consumer law.

5. Urgent legal matters

A customer should not buy a pilot service as a substitute for urgent legal or regulated professional assistance. If the matter is clearly outside the pilot scope, DOXA-SURE should prioritise an appropriate referral rather than selling an unsuitable service.

6. How to request a cancellation or refund

Contact the DOXA-SURE WhatsApp Business Assistant at 066 298 2213 with the customer name, service purchased, payment date and a brief reason for the request. Do not send banking passwords, PINs or login credentials.

7. Payment method

Until an approved automated payment gateway is connected, DOXA-SURE should clearly tell customers how payment will be collected and should issue or retain reasonable proof of the transaction. The website must not imply automated payment processing where none exists.

8. Review

This is an interim founding-pilot policy and should receive professional consumer-law review as the business scales.
