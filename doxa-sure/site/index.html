FAQ

Know exactly what the pilot is — and what it is not.

Is DOXA-SURE an insurance policy?

No. The founding pilot is an assistance and case-organisation service. Insurance products are not being sold through this pilot.

Does DOXA-SURE guarantee that my house or car will be saved?

No. No lender concession, enforcement pause, restructuring or asset-rescue outcome is guaranteed.

What happens if I already have court or repossession documents?

Use the risk check for orientation, but seek suitably qualified professional help urgently because legal deadlines may continue to run.

What is the R199 Rescue Readiness Pack?

It is a structured preparation service that helps organise the factual position, document inventory, communication timeline and next-step preparation.

What is the R99/month Shield?

It is a founding-pilot rescue-readiness membership. It is not insurance cover and does not pay arrears or claims.
