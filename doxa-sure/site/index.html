Important boundaries

Know what the pilot is — and what it is not.

Is DOXA-SURE an insurance policy?

No. The founding pilot is an assistance, early-warning and case-organisation service. It is not an insurance policy or insurer.

Does DOXA-SURE guarantee that my house or car will be saved?

No. No lender concession, enforcement pause, restructuring or asset-rescue outcome is guaranteed.

What if I already have summons, court or repossession documents?

Use the Help Desk for orientation, but seek suitably qualified professional help urgently because legal deadlines may continue to run.

Does the Help Desk replace an attorney, debt counsellor or financial adviser?

No. The on-site assistant provides general pilot information and early-warning guidance only. Regulated or legal matters must be handled by the appropriate authorised professional.
