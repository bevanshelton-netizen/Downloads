Pilot Terms

Founding-pilot terms for the DOXA-SURE assistance and case-organisation service.

1. What DOXA-SURE is

DOXA-SURE is a bootstrap assistance, early-warning and case-organisation pilot. It helps customers organise factual information, understand urgency, prepare documents and communication timelines, and identify when an authorised professional or provider process may be appropriate.

2. What DOXA-SURE is not

The pilot is not an insurance policy, insurer, legal practice, debt-counselling service, debt-cancellation service or financial-product advisory service. It does not guarantee that an asset can be saved and it does not stop legal or contractual deadlines.

3. Founding offers

The Free Asset Risk Check is R0. The Rescue Readiness Pack is R199 once-off during the founding pilot. DOXA-SURE Shield is R99 per month during the founding pilot.

4. Urgent matters

If a customer has received summons, court papers, a repossession notice, a sale-in-execution notice or another time-sensitive legal document, suitably qualified professional assistance should be sought urgently. Buying or using a DOXA-SURE pilot service must not delay that step.

5. On-site Help Desk

The automated DOXA-SURE Help Desk provides general pilot information and early-warning orientation only. It does not replace an attorney, registered debt counsellor, insurer, financial adviser or other regulated professional.

6. Payments

Payment processing is not represented as active unless the website expressly states otherwise. Live PayFast checkout will only be enabled after the existing merchant account completes live verification. Current cancellation and refund rules are set out in the Refund & Cancellation Policy.

7. Privacy

Customers should share only information reasonably necessary for the requested service and must not provide banking passwords, PINs, security answers or online-banking login credentials. See the Privacy & POPIA Notice.

8. Contact

Questions about the pilot can be asked through the on-site DOXA-SURE Help Desk on the main website.
