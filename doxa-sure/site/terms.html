Pilot Terms

Founding-pilot terms for the DOXA-SURE assistance and case-organisation service. Version 1 — 25 August 2026.

1. What DOXA-SURE is

DOXA-SURE is currently a bootstrap assistance, early-warning and case-organisation pilot. It helps customers organise factual information, understand urgency, prepare documents and communication timelines, and identify when an authorised professional or provider process may be appropriate.

2. What DOXA-SURE is not

The pilot is not an insurance policy, insurer, legal practice, debt-counselling service, debt-cancellation service or financial-product advisory service. It does not guarantee that a home, vehicle, business asset or other property can be saved, and it does not stop legal or contractual deadlines.

3. Founding offers

The Free Asset Risk Check is R0. The Rescue Readiness Pack is offered at R199 once-off during the founding pilot. DOXA-SURE Shield is offered at R99 per month during the founding pilot. Pricing may change for future customers, but any change will be communicated before a new paid period begins.

4. Customer responsibility

Customers must provide information they reasonably believe is accurate and must not provide banking passwords, PINs, security answers or online-banking login credentials. Customers remain responsible for decisions, payments, correspondence and legal deadlines relating to their lenders, insurers, attorneys, debt counsellors or other providers.

5. Urgent legal matters

If a customer has received summons, court papers, a repossession notice, a sale-in-execution notice or another time-sensitive legal document, qualified professional assistance should be sought urgently. Buying or using a DOXA-SURE pilot service must not delay that step.

6. Referrals

Where regulated or professional help is required, DOXA-SURE may help prepare a referral file or point the customer toward an appropriately authorised professional. The professional remains independently responsible for their advice and services.

7. Payments and refunds

Payment processing is not represented as automated unless the website expressly states otherwise. Current cancellation and refund rules are set out in the Refund & Cancellation Policy.

8. Privacy

Personal information must be handled in accordance with the DOXA-SURE Privacy & POPIA Notice. Customers should share only information reasonably necessary for the requested service.

9. Pilot status

This is an evolving founding pilot. Features may be improved, changed or withdrawn as the service is tested. These terms are intended as a practical interim customer notice and should receive professional legal/compliance review before the business expands into regulated insurance, advice or debt-counselling activities.
