# DOXA-SURE Brokerage #1 Outreach Script

## Short introduction

Hello [Name]. I’m reaching out regarding DOXA-SURE, a new broker-agnostic customer-protection and brokerage technology platform. The model is simple: customers come through one doorway, explain what they need, and DOXA-SURE routes them only to a participating brokerage whose verified FSP authority and service scope fit the request.

We are opening a controlled Founding 50 pilot and are looking for the first brokerage partner to help prove the operating model with us.

DOXA-SURE does not borrow or present a brokerage's FSP licence as its own. The participating brokerage remains responsible for regulated advice and intermediary services. Our role is the marketplace, matching, customer journey and workflow technology.

The founding offer is R0 joining fee, R0 monthly platform fee for the first 3 months, and R50 per qualified lead the brokerage accepts.

Would you be open to a 45-minute discussion including your Key Individual or compliance representative so we can test whether the model fits your brokerage?

## Meeting agenda

1. Brokerage/FSP scope and target customer profile
2. DOXA-SURE customer and broker journey
3. Which services stay with DOXA-SURE vs the FSP
4. Matching rules and customer transparency
5. Data/POPIA and complaints allocation
6. Commercial model
7. First 10–25 lead controlled pilot
8. Compliance sign-off steps before production

## What to request before activation

- Brokerage legal/trading name
- FSP number
- Key Individual/decision-maker
- Compliance contact
- Relevant categories/subcategories
- Provinces/customer types served
- Insurer/product access relevant to the pilot
- Capacity/response targets
- Preferred partnership tier

Do not request customer data, adviser passwords, insurer portal credentials or other sensitive access during initial brokerage outreach.
