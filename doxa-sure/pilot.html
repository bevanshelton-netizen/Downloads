Questions people ask

Clear answers before you start.

Is DOXA-SURE an insurance company?

No. The founding pilot is a protection-readiness and case-organisation service. Insurance products must only be offered through the appropriate authorised structure.

Can DOXA-SURE stop a bank or cancel my debt?

No. DOXA-SURE does not promise to cancel debt, stop lawful enforcement or guarantee that an asset can be saved. The purpose is to help people act earlier and prepare the facts properly.

What if I have received a summons or repossession notice?

Treat it as urgent. Legal deadlines can continue to run. Seek suitably qualified professional assistance without waiting for the pilot service.

Why check credit-life or payment-protection cover?

Some credit agreements may include cover relevant to events such as retrenchment, illness, disability or death. The existence and terms of any cover must be confirmed from the actual policy or agreement.

Do I need to send passwords or banking PINs?

No. DOXA-SURE never needs your banking password, PIN or online-banking login details for the pilot.
