# DOXA-SURE Brokerage Partnership Proposal

## Purpose

DOXA-SURE is building a broker-agnostic customer protection and brokerage technology marketplace. The objective is to give consumers one clear entry point, then route them to an appropriately authorised participating brokerage for any regulated financial service.

DOXA-SURE does not seek to rent, borrow or present a partner's FSP licence as its own. Each participating brokerage remains responsible for the regulated advice/intermediary services it provides, unless a separate lawful representative arrangement has been documented and approved through the brokerage's compliance structure.

## Proposed partnership model

### DOXA-SURE provides
- consumer acquisition and education;
- non-advisory needs-intake and early-warning tools;
- broker matching technology;
- lead and workflow routing;
- document-readiness and case-organisation tools;
- customer status, response-time and service tracking;
- optional white-label customer and broker portals;
- audit trails for marketplace routing decisions.

### The brokerage provides
- its authorised FSP identity and disclosures;
- confirmation of licence categories/subcategories and service scope;
- key individual/compliance oversight;
- appropriately appointed representatives/advisers;
- advice, quotations and intermediary services within its authority;
- insurer/product relationships;
- regulated record keeping and complaints handling for services it provides;
- approval of any regulated product wording used in its customer journey.

## Customer journey

1. Customer enters DOXA-SURE.
2. DOXA-SURE captures the customer's broad need without giving regulated advice.
3. The matching engine filters participating brokerages by verified eligibility and service scope.
4. The customer is shown the brokerage/FSP responsible for the regulated service.
5. The brokerage accepts or declines the opportunity.
6. Advice, quotations and intermediary services continue under the brokerage's compliant process.
7. DOXA-SURE may continue to provide non-regulated workflow, readiness and customer-experience tools.

Paid plan level must never override eligibility.

## Partnership options

### Option A — Referral/technology partner
DOXA-SURE remains outside the regulated advice process and acts as the marketplace, technology and customer-intake layer. The brokerage performs all regulated financial services.

### Option B — White-label technology partner
The brokerage uses a branded DOXA-SURE-powered customer and staff experience while retaining its FSP identity, compliance obligations and regulated process.

### Option C — Formal representative structure
Where appropriate, a brokerage may consider a lawful juristic-representative or other representative arrangement. This is not created by this proposal or an MOU alone. It requires the brokerage's formal compliance, fit-and-proper, supervision, representative-register and contractual processes to be completed before regulated activity begins.

## Verification before activation

DOXA-SURE will require, at minimum:
- registered/trading name;
- FSP number;
- FSCA status check;
- authorised categories/subcategories relevant to requested services;
- key individual/contact details;
- compliance contact;
- complaints contact;
- adviser/representative operating model;
- provinces/areas served;
- customer types served;
- declared insurer/product access;
- service capacity and response-time commitments;
- data-sharing and POPIA contacts;
- signed commercial and compliance documents.

## Non-negotiable legal boundaries

- No FSP licence is rented, borrowed or presented as belonging to DOXA-SURE.
- No customer-facing match is activated solely because a brokerage paid a higher fee.
- No brokerage is shown as verified before its status and service scope have been checked.
- DOXA-SURE does not guarantee a quote, policy, claim outcome, debt solution or asset-rescue outcome.
- Debt counselling remains within the applicable NCR framework and is not authorised by an FSP relationship.
- Legal representation remains outside the brokerage/FSP relationship unless provided by a suitably authorised legal practitioner.
- Binder functions are outside this proposal unless separately and lawfully contracted with the relevant insurer/binder structure.

## Pilot objective

The first pilot should onboard one compliant brokerage, configure only the service categories that brokerage is authorised and operationally ready to handle, test a small number of customer matches, measure response quality and audit every routing decision before national scaling.

## Proposed next step

A 45-minute scoping meeting with the brokerage's decision-maker and compliance/Key Individual representative, followed by:
1. FSP and scope verification;
2. commercial model selection;
3. compliance/data-sharing review;
4. signed MOU and any required formal agreements;
5. controlled pilot activation.
