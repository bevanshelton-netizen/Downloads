# Legal review required before paid public launch

DOXA-SURE v1 intentionally stays on the assistance/case-organisation side of the line.

Before charging the public or presenting regulated partner services as part of a packaged financial product, obtain South African professional review covering at least:

- FAIS/FSP implications of the exact customer journey and remuneration model
- Insurance Act / insurer terminology and underwriting-partner wording
- National Credit Act boundaries, especially debt counselling and credit-provider engagement
- Attorney referral and legal-expense wording
- POPIA notices, consent, retention and operator agreements
- Consumer Protection Act terms and cancellation/refund wording
- Marketing claims around SAVE MY ASSET and what DOXA-SURE can and cannot promise

The product must never state or imply that pressing SAVE MY ASSET automatically suspends legal proceedings, prevents repossession, stops a sale in execution, or guarantees debt restructuring.
