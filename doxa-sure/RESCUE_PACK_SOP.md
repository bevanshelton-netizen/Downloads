# DOXA-SURE Rescue Readiness Pack — Manual SOP

## Purpose
Deliver the R199 founding-pilot service safely and consistently without expensive software.

## Service boundary
The Rescue Readiness Pack is administrative case organisation and referral preparation. It is not:
- insurance cover or a claim decision;
- legal advice or representation;
- debt counselling;
- financial-product advice;
- a guarantee that a lender will restructure an account or suspend enforcement.

If the facts indicate urgent legal/enforcement activity, do not delay the customer from seeking suitably qualified professional help.

## Intake
Collect only what is necessary:
- customer name and contact details;
- asset type;
- lender/provider name;
- outstanding balance if known;
- monthly instalment if known;
- number and amount of missed payments;
- date of next payment;
- income-shock category;
- whether credit-life/payment protection is known, unknown or believed absent;
- list of formal notices/documents received;
- chronology of lender/provider communications supplied by the customer.

Never request passwords, PINs, online-banking credentials or security answers.

## Pack contents
Each completed pack should contain:
1. **Case cover sheet** — DOXA-SURE case number, date/time opened, asset type and urgency.
2. **Asset-risk summary** — factual balances, instalments, arrears and payment dates supplied by the customer.
3. **Income-shock summary** — factual description and date of the change.
4. **Document inventory** — present / missing / requested.
5. **Credit-life identification status** — yes / no / unknown. This is document identification only, not policy interpretation or advice.
6. **Communication timeline** — calls, emails, letters and meetings supplied by the customer.
7. **Priority checklist** — administrative actions and referral-preparation steps.
8. **Referral sheet** — where required, identify the category of authorised professional needed and record the referral.
9. **Customer acknowledgement** — confirms the service boundary and that legal/credit deadlines continue to run.

## Urgency classification
### Green
No missed payment, no material income shock, no formal collection/legal stage.

### Amber
One missed payment or material income reduction but no formal enforcement documents.

### Red
Two or more missed payments, severe income shock, or Section 129/formal default notice.

### Critical
Summons, court papers, repossession process, execution/auction process, or another time-sensitive legal event.

The classification is triage only; it is not legal advice.

## Manual fulfilment workflow
1. Run the free Asset Risk Check.
2. Explain the paid pack and service boundary.
3. Confirm payment through the approved business payment method once available; until then, record the request but do not claim payment automation exists.
4. Open the Rescue Case.
5. Freeze the factual intake snapshot.
6. Build the document inventory.
7. Build the communication chronology.
8. Identify missing information.
9. Produce the priority checklist.
10. Escalate/referral-prep if authorised professional input is required.
11. Deliver the pack to the customer through the approved secure channel.
12. Record delivery date/time and any follow-up required.
13. Ask for anonymous feedback only after delivery.

## Critical-case rule
Where there is a summons, court date, repossession appointment or sale-in-execution/auction process, the first objective is not a DOXA-SURE sale. The first objective is to make the urgency clear and route the customer toward qualified help without suggesting that DOXA-SURE has stopped any legal process.

## Quality check before delivery
- facts are clearly attributed to the customer or source document;
- no invented legal conclusions;
- no promise of outcome;
- no unverified statement that credit-life will pay;
- no statement that a lender must agree to a concession unless confirmed by qualified advice;
- all missing documents are labelled missing, not assumed;
- regulated referrals are clearly separated from DOXA-SURE administrative assistance;
- customer-sensitive information is not stored in GitHub.
