# NexTradeFinX V5 — Initial Market Data Decision

## Preferred MVP / shadow provider: Twelve Data

For the first commercial-grade shadow phase, Twelve Data is the preferred provider candidate because one integration can cover forex, crypto, commodities, US markets and the Johannesburg Stock Exchange, while its business plans explicitly address commercial and external-display use.

### Recommended starting tier

**Venture business plan** is the practical baseline to negotiate for NexTradeFinX. Current published pricing is approximately US$499/month month-to-month (or about US$414/month when billed annually), before exchange-specific add-ons or redistribution rights.

Why:
- 70+ markets
- external display access
- commodities
- real-time forex and crypto
- global equities/EOD coverage
- JSE is listed as available with Venture as the minimum business tier

## Important licensing boundary

A business subscription does not automatically mean unrestricted redistribution. Twelve Data states that redistribution can require a separate agreement and that some non-US regions require additional approval for commercial use.

Therefore the first live-shadow phase should remain internal/non-client-visible until our contract explicitly covers the intended display, derived-data and redistribution use.

## Massive position

Massive remains a strong secondary candidate for deep US-market data. Its current Stocks Business plan is materially more expensive (about US$2,499/month) and is better suited to a later phase where full US history, trades, quotes and high-volume streaming justify the cost.

## Contract questions before activation

1. Is internal model training/backtesting permitted?
2. May we retain historical bars in our own warehouse?
3. Are derived probabilities and AI explanations considered derived data we may display?
4. What rights are required to show raw or delayed prices to NexTradeFinX users?
5. What JSE delay/coverage applies to our account?
6. Are per-user exchange fees or reporting obligations triggered?
7. What attribution is required?
8. What additional agreement is required for white-label or redistribution?
9. Can the same contract support mobile, web and API-delivered client experiences?
10. What data may be used for automated research versus future execution systems?

## Activation recommendation

Do not buy the most expensive data stack yet. Start with the smallest business agreement that legally covers internal live-shadow research and JSE/global coverage. Upgrade redistribution/display rights only when the client-facing market-data experience is ready.
