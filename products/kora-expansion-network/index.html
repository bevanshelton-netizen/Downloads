Rights-first by design

Every content item is designed to carry a rights record: owner → licence → territory → platform → term → permitted use → evidence → expiry. No rights record means no publication.

KORA does not authorise unauthorised rebroadcasting of third-party channels, films, music, sports feeds or other protected content. Regulatory classification and licensing requirements are assessed for each service and market before launch.

South Africa regulatory posture

ICASA's OTT regulatory work is evolving. KORA therefore treats regulatory classification as an onboarding gate rather than assuming that an online service is automatically exempt or automatically licensed.
