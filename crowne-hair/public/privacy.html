Privacy Policy

We collect only information reasonably needed to respond to a reservation, confirm an order and provide customer service. This can include your name, email address, phone number, product preferences, notes, campaign source and reservation reference.

How we use information

We use it to contact you about the requested product, confirm stock and specifications, arrange delivery, process an approved payment flow, prevent fraud and keep transaction or support records where required.

Marketing

Marketing messages are optional. We only record marketing consent when you select the consent option. Reservation and order communications are separate from marketing.

Payment data

Crowne by Netty does not ask you to submit full card details, PINs, CVVs, banking passwords or OTPs into this website. Online payment is not active during the reservation stage. Any future payment flow will identify Netty’s confirmed merchant details and approved payment provider before payment.

Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers involved in hosting, payment, fraud prevention, delivery or fulfilment where necessary to complete the requested service. We do not sell customer conversation or order data as an advertising list.

Your request

You may ask us to correct or delete information where applicable, subject to records we are legally or operationally required to retain.
