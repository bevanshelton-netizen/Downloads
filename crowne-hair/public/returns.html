Returns & Refunds

Important: Hair products can be hygiene-sensitive. The exact return eligibility for a confirmed product is disclosed before payment.

Before payment

During the reservation stage, no return issue arises because no sale has been completed. We first confirm the exact product and applicable return conditions.

Incorrect, damaged or defective goods

If an item arrives incorrect, damaged or defective, contact CROWNÉ Hair promptly and keep the packaging and supporting photographs where practical. Your statutory rights are not excluded.

Change-of-mind and hygiene-sensitive goods

Eligibility can depend on whether seals, lace, packaging, tags or hygiene protections have been removed, altered, cut, installed, worn, treated or otherwise changed. The applicable conditions will be shown with the confirmed order before payment.

Refund method

Where a refund is approved, it is processed through an appropriate verified payment route. We will never request a banking password, PIN or OTP to issue a refund.
