IZAKHONO ONE opens a focused “Founding 40” growth sprint for South African SMEs

The campaign starts with a free three-minute Business Health Check and moves qualified businesses into a hands-on implementation programme covering the operating systems many SMEs struggle to assemble.

At a glance
  • Free Business Health Check as the public entry point
  • Limited Founding 40 implementation cycle
  • R25,000 once-off programme fee for participating businesses
  • Focus on web presence, lead capture, CRM, payments, bookkeeping readiness, AI-assisted workflows and launch marketing setup
  • South African SME audience first, with wider African expansion possible later

The problem the campaign addresses

Many owner-managed businesses run customer follow-up, quoting, collections, payments, bookkeeping, marketing and decision-making through disconnected tools and manual processes. IZAKHONO ONE is being positioned around that operational pain rather than around software features alone.

The free Business Health Check asks owners a short set of practical questions about pricing, receivables, forward cash flow, bookkeeping, tax readiness, payroll, protection, sales pipeline and major spending decisions. It returns an immediate control score and priority actions.

Founding 40

Businesses that identify material gaps and want implementation support can apply for the Founding 40 Business Growth Sprint. The programme is limited to 40 businesses in the current launch cycle so that delivery capacity remains controlled.

The implementation offer is designed to bring together a practical SME operating stack: professional web presence and lead capture, business email, CRM and follow-up setup, payment enablement using approved infrastructure where suitable, bookkeeping and tax-readiness setup, AI-assisted workflows, and a launch marketing starter pack.

For entrepreneur communities and media

IZAKHONO AFRICA is inviting entrepreneur networks, chambers, accelerators, SME-support organisations, accounting communities and business media to distribute the free Health Check, host a Business Health Clinic, or explore a founder-focused collaboration.
