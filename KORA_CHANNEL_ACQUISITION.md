# KORA Cable — Channel Acquisition Registry

## Mission
Build KORA as an African-first digital cable / FAST ecosystem: one EPG, live TV, VOD, music, creator channels, events, radio/audio and global distribution.

## Acquisition rule
No channel is published until KORA has verified the rights, territory, term, technical feed, EPG metadata, advertising/monetisation rights and contractual permission to distribute it.

## Priority partner pipeline

### Tier 1 — African channel aggregation / distribution
1. Sentech / FreeVision Play — strategic South African broadcast, OTT and infrastructure partner. Explore licensed channel carriage, EPG/data exchange, OTT/FAST technology, broadcaster onboarding and distribution.
2. AFREE TV — African FAST/OTT ecosystem with live TV, radio, VOD and podcasts. Explore reciprocal channel carriage and broadcaster distribution.
3. The Africa Channel — African-focused international television network. Explore channel carriage, VOD/catalogue licensing, diaspora distribution and co-marketing.
4. FAST Channels TV — 80+ FAST channels and a dedicated African catalogue. Priority channel requests: Aforevo 2, AMusic Channel, Mama Benz TV, Nigbati TV, R2TV, Teen Africa, X2DTV, Nolly Africa, 4ACETV AFROBEAT, Plug TV and additional African/Black Cinema channels subject to territory clearance.
5. OTTera — FAST/OTT technology, channel distribution, monetisation and StreamBridge. Explore technical + distribution partnership and African channel aggregation.

## Channel onboarding pipeline
TARGET → CONTACT → QUALIFY → RIGHTS HOLDER → RIGHTS CHECK → TERRITORY → TECHNICAL CHECK → COMMERCIAL MODEL → CONTRACT → INGEST → QC → EPG → TEST → PUBLISH → MONETISE → ANALYTICS → RENEW / EXPAND

## Product differentiation
KORA Cable is not a copy of traditional cable. It combines:
- Linear TV + FAST + VOD in one guide
- One-account access across TV, music, audio, creators and events
- African-first programming and languages
- Creator-owned channels and Creator Hubs
- KORA Share and referral distribution
- KORA Pay: subscription, PPV, memberships, tips and creator earnings
- KORA Ads and sponsorship
- AI-powered discovery, translation, metadata and accessibility, subject to rights
- Offline/low-connectivity resilience as the platform develops
- Africa-to-world distribution through KORA Global

## Immediate commercial objective
Secure a launch portfolio of licensed third-party channels while simultaneously developing KORA-owned channels so KORA controls the customer relationship, EPG, audience, monetisation and African original channel identity.
