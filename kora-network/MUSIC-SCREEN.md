# KORA MUSIC SCREEN

KORA is the video/film surface for the ALLEGRO creator career engine.

Supported handoff types:
- music video
- live session
- concert film
- artist documentary
- revival/comeback documentary
- tour diary
- behind-the-scenes series
- music biopic
- fictional music movie
- launch film
- interview special

## Career use

**Launch** — debut film, first music video, first live session, artist-origin documentary.

**Revive** — catalogue story, legacy documentary, anniversary concert, archive restoration, oral-history/interview special.

**Relaunch** — comeback documentary, new-era visual campaign, reintroduction film, live comeback session, new music video.

## Rights boundary

A public upload is never considered cleared simply because the artist supplied the file.

Every handoff must confirm music/master/performer/visual rights. Documentaries additionally require interview releases, archival-footage and still/photo clearance. Music movies/biopics also require script, cast and location rights.

The API creates KORA content only as **draft + pending moderation + rights review**. It does not auto-publish.

## Security

ALLEGRO uses a server-side integration key. The key must never be shipped to the browser.

Artists must explicitly link their ALLEGRO creator reference to their authenticated KORA creator profile before a handoff can be created.
