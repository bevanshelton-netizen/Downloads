# KORA Content & Safety Policy

## Core rule
KORA is a family-safe African entertainment network. Pornography and explicit sexual content are prohibited.

## Not allowed
- Pornography or content whose primary purpose is sexual arousal.
- Explicit depictions of sexual acts or explicit nudity presented sexually.
- Sexual exploitation, non-consensual intimate media, sexual extortion or trafficking content.
- Any sexual content involving minors, including sexualised depictions or grooming.
- Paid sexual services, explicit sexual-content marketplaces or links intended to evade this policy.

## Allowed with ratings and context
Non-explicit romance, kissing, relationship themes, culturally relevant storytelling, educational health material, documentary/news context and mature dramatic themes may be allowed when they are not pornographic and are appropriately rated.

## Enforcement
1. Automated pre-screening on upload.
2. Human review for flagged or high-risk content.
3. Creator self-rating plus platform rating.
4. Viewer reporting and rapid takedown path.
5. Strikes, monetisation suspension and account termination for repeat or severe violations.
6. Immediate preservation/escalation processes where required by law for child-safety material.

## Product controls
- Age ratings on productions and episodes.
- Kids profile restricted to approved catalogue.
- Parental PIN support.
- Search/recommendation filtering.
- Advertiser brand-safety exclusions.
