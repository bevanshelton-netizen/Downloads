# TOUR2SCREEN™

**Every tour is also a media business.**

KORA Tour2Screen helps touring musicians and bands capture the road professionally and turn it into multiple protected revenue assets.

## The rule

Do not finish a tour with only memories and phone clips.

A properly documented tour can produce:

1. **Tour diary series** — episodic ad-supported or premium viewing.
2. **Concert film** — free premiere, premium release or pay-per-view.
3. **Backstage / rehearsal content** — fan-access series.
4. **Live sessions** — individual performance assets.
5. **Short-form clips** — audience growth and sponsor fulfilment.
6. **Fan stories** — community-led tour narrative.
7. **Tour documentary** — post-tour feature film.
8. **Sponsor features** — approved branded segments.
9. **Ticket upsells** — venue ticket + stream/replay bundle.
10. **Post-tour catalogue** — evergreen monetisation after the buses stop.

## Career pathways

### LAUNCH
A first tour becomes the artist's origin story.

Capture:
- first headline nights;
- rehearsals;
- first fan reactions;
- travel;
- failures and breakthroughs;
- debut concert film.

### GROW
Use repeated touring to build a recurring KORA audience and sponsor proposition.

### REVIVE
Legacy artists can turn archive stories, old venues, reunion rehearsals and catalogue performances into a revival documentary.

### RELAUNCH
A comeback tour becomes a structured relaunch campaign:
- "where have they been?";
- new music;
- old hits revisited;
- comeback rehearsals;
- return-to-stage film;
- documentary + concert release.

## Suggested tour media package

At every stop:

**Before show**
- city arrival;
- venue intro;
- rehearsal/soundcheck;
- local culture/food;
- sponsor-approved moment.

**During show**
- selected cleared songs;
- crowd energy;
- backstage transition;
- fan vox pops where permission/notice is handled.

**After show**
- artist debrief;
- fan reactions;
- next-city teaser;
- performance highlight.

This creates a repeatable 8–20 minute episode without forcing the artist to invent a new format every day.

## Rights Shield

A tour camera creates legal obligations.

Before commercial publication, KORA should track:
- venue filming permission;
- promoter permission;
- band/member/performer releases;
- audience filming notices where appropriate;
- music composition/master rights;
- guest artist consent;
- sponsor/brand permissions;
- minors;
- archive/third-party footage;
- photographer/videographer ownership;
- location permissions.

A sold ticket does not automatically give KORA unlimited image/likeness or film rights.

## Crew-light version

Artists do not need a film crew at every stop.

Minimum viable road kit:
- 1 good smartphone/camera;
- wireless lav/shotgun microphone;
- small stabiliser/tripod;
- portable light;
- daily media backup;
- one designated tour-content person;
- standard shot list.

KORA can later offer approved production partners and editing packages.

## Creator protection

Raw footage remains a valuable asset. The artist/rights holder should know:
- who owns the footage;
- who may edit it;
- who may monetise it;
- KORA's licence scope;
- revenue split;
- takedown/termination rights;
- archive retention.

Never trade permanent master/film ownership merely for basic filming assistance without informed review.
