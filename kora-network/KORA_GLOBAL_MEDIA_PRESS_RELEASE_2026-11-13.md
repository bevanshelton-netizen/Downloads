KORA NETWORK INTRODUCES AN AFRICAN-FIRST DIGITAL HOME FOR STORIES, CREATORS AND CULTURAL EXPRESSION

Johannesburg, South Africa — KORA Network, an African media and digital distribution initiative owned by IZAKHONO AFRICA (Pty) Ltd, is building a platform designed to bring African films, series, documentaries, music, audiobooks and creator-led channels closer to audiences across Africa and the global diaspora.

Operating through Kora Stream Africa, KORA's purpose is captured in its promise: “Africa's Stories. One Home.” The platform provides a digital destination where viewers can discover African storytelling shaped by the continent's many cultures, languages, experiences and creative voices.

WHAT KORA IS

KORA is being developed as more than a conventional streaming destination. Its wider ambition is to connect content, creators, audiences, brands, businesses and distribution partners in one African-centred media ecosystem.

The current Kora Stream Africa experience offers a catalogue of African films, series, documentaries, audiobooks, Kora Originals, Kids & Family titles and approved partner-channel content. Viewers can create a free account and explore the available catalogue. Kora Kids is a separate family experience featuring titles approved for children, supported by parent-managed controls.

A PLATFORM FOR AFRICAN CREATORS AND RIGHTS HOLDERS

KORA invites filmmakers, producers, broadcasters, authors and authorised rights holders to enquire about submitting suitable work or applying to operate a partner channel. Submissions are reviewed, and applicants must own or control the rights required to make content available.

The initiative aims to create additional routes to discovery and distribution for African creative work, while respecting ownership, licensing agreements and the rights of creators.

CONNECTING STORIES WITH BUSINESS AND OPPORTUNITY

KORA's longer-term commercial vision includes advertising, sponsorship, branded storytelling, enterprise visibility programmes, production partnerships and expanded distribution. The goal is to create responsible commercial pathways that can support content creation and help brands and organisations connect with relevant audiences through African stories.

These commercial capabilities are being developed in stages. KORA is engaging potential advertising, technology, production and distribution partners; it is not representing all planned advertising or live-stream monetisation features as already operational.

WHAT KORA HOPES TO ACHIEVE

KORA's ambitions include:
• Make African films, series, documentaries and other creative work easier to discover.
• Give African creators and rights holders additional opportunities to present and distribute their work.
• Connect audiences on the continent with African stories and with the diaspora worldwide.
• Build partnerships with broadcasters, production companies, technology providers, brands, agencies and institutions.
• Develop sustainable commercial models through advertising, sponsorship, content partnerships and distribution.
• Contribute to a stronger, more connected African creative economy and help African stories travel internationally.

A CALL TO MEDIA, CREATORS AND STRATEGIC PARTNERS

KORA welcomes conversations with media organisations interested in covering the platform and the wider African creative economy; creators and rights holders interested in content submissions; broadcasters and distributors exploring content partnerships; and brands, agencies and institutions interested in future advertising, sponsorship and enterprise collaborations.

ABOUT KORA NETWORK

KORA Network is an African-first media and digital distribution initiative owned by IZAKHONO AFRICA (Pty) Ltd. Through Kora Stream Africa, it is creating a home for African films, series, documentaries, audiobooks, family content and creator-led channels. Its wider vision is to connect African stories, creators, audiences, brands and distribution partners through a sustainable media ecosystem.
