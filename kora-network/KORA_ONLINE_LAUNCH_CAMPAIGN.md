# KORA Online Launch — Pan-Platform Campaign Plan
**Prepared:** 13 November 2026  
**Business owner:** IZAKHONO AFRICA (Pty) Ltd  
**Campaign status:** planning and asset production; no public launch date or social account ownership verified.

## Campaign ambition
Launch KORA as an African-first entertainment, creator and advertising network. Build awareness, collect qualified audience interest, recruit creators and partner channels, and acquire the first paying advertisers. All channels must direct audiences to a single verified KORA destination with campaign tracking.

## Release approach: two stages

### Stage 1 — Awareness and controlled acquisition
Can begin only with a verified landing page, working enquiry/waitlist capture, privacy notice, support contact and functioning analytics.
- Introduce KORA and its African storytelling proposition.
- Invite viewers to register interest / join controlled launch.
- Recruit filmmakers, rights holders, creators and channel partners.
- Invite brands and agencies to request an advertising pack or campaign consultation.
- Publish demonstrations only where the demonstrated feature is actually working.
- Avoid "watch now", "live now", audience-size, catalogue-size or monetisation claims that have not been verified.

### Stage 2 — Public launch
Open only after signed release acceptance for streaming playback, live channels, mobile/desktop experience, user registration, support, content rights, child safety, privacy, moderation, incident response, payments and ad delivery. Launch date is a release decision, not a marketing guess.

## Campaign message architecture

**Master line:** Africa's Stories. One Home.

**Viewer proposition:** Discover African films, series, music, documentaries and creator-led channels.

**Creator proposition:** Bring your stories and channel to a platform built to showcase African voices. Submission and publication remain subject to rights and editorial review.

**Advertiser proposition:** Put your brand alongside African stories and audiences. Enquire about video campaigns, live-channel sponsorships and managed creative services. Do not promise audience reach or ad delivery metrics until verified.

**Partner proposition:** Help distribute, produce, license or monetise African content through a transparent partnership.

## Four-week campaign cadence (relative to approved start)

**Week 1 — Tease and explain**
- Master brand reveal and founder message.
- Website landing page and tracking validation.
- Publish "What is KORA?" explainer.
- Start opt-in viewer, creator and advertiser lead capture.

**Week 2 — Demonstrate**
- Release verified product walkthroughs.
- Feature approved creators and content partners.
- Publish advertiser proposition and book discovery calls.
- Begin small, capped video-view and lead-generation tests.

**Week 3 — Mobilise**
- Creator cross-posting and partner announcements.
- Press outreach and interviews.
- Community Q&A / controlled live showcase if streaming and moderation are verified.
- Retarget consenting visitors with clear frequency limits.

**Week 4 — Launch window**
- Only after signed release gates: launch film premiere, coordinated posts, press coverage, creator amplification, email/WhatsApp announcements and paid campaigns.
- Daily issue monitoring, support coverage and campaign pacing.
- Publish only verified availability and product claims.

## Audience and campaign segmentation
- Viewers: African entertainment fans and diaspora audiences.
- Creators: filmmakers, producers, musicians, educators, channel operators and rights holders.
- Advertisers: SMEs, consumer brands, retailers, telecoms, financial services, education, tourism, entertainment and agencies, subject to category approval.
- Partners: broadcasters, content owners, production houses, ad-tech providers, distributors and institutions.

Keep Kora Kids and family content promotion separate from behavioural ad targeting. Do not use child profile data for marketing segmentation.

## Conversion paths
- Viewer: social/video → KORA landing page → interest registration / account → first successful play.
- Creator: creator post / search / partner referral → creator information → submission/application → rights review → onboarding.
- Advertiser: LinkedIn / agency / direct outreach → advertiser landing page → qualified enquiry → discovery call → proposal → signed order → cleared funds → approved campaign → verified report.
- Partner: press/industry referral → partnership brief → discovery → technical/legal/commercial diligence → pilot agreement.

## Measurement
Report separately by channel and audience segment:
- reach and frequency;
- video views and completion rate;
- click-through rate and landing-page conversion;
- viewer registrations and first successful plays;
- creator applications and rights-approved submissions;
- qualified advertiser leads, meetings, proposals, signed orders and collected revenue;
- cost per qualified lead and campaign return, only where attribution is defensible.

Never use impressions, followers, registrations or video starts as substitutes for paying customers or active viewers.

## Governance and release blockers
Before Stage 1: verify official social accounts, destination URLs, analytics consent, forms, inbox ownership, support routing and approved claims.
Before Stage 2: complete the KORA release gate checklist covering live/VOD streaming, playback across devices, registration, payments, ad insertion, campaign funding, content licensing, child safety, POPIA/privacy, moderation, incident response, backups and support operations.
Do not claim public launch, live video advertising or commercial readiness based on a deployment status alone.

## Immediate execution sequence
1. Audit and confirm all KORA-owned social accounts, access and recovery.
2. Confirm one canonical landing page and separate viewer / creator / advertiser conversions.
3. Produce the master launch film and cutdowns.
4. Build the content calendar and publishing workflow.
5. Prepare the advertiser pack and lead pipeline.
6. Recruit launch partners and creators with written permissions for cross-promotion.
7. Run a small test campaign; validate tracking and lead handling.
8. Launch Stage 1; hold Stage 2 until release gates pass.
