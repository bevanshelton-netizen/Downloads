# YHVH GOSPEL TV — Global Distribution Route Policy

## Purpose

YHVH GOSPEL TV is designed for worldwide Gospel distribution through lawful, resilient routes controlled by IZAKHONO.

## Route order

1. IZAKHONO-owned web/distribution infrastructure where available and authorised.
2. Verified Christian broadcaster or distribution partner with territorial rights.
3. Licensed satellite, OTT, FAST, mobile or digital distribution.
4. Authorised local distributor or broadcaster.
5. Approved external fallback infrastructure for a route already authorised for that territory.

## Secure private network

IZAKHONO may operate a private VPN/mesh for authorised employees, contractors, studios, production sites, engineering systems and partner operations. It is an operational security layer, not a mechanism for evading local law, licensing, censorship controls or territorial rights.

## Failover rule

A technical outage does not change legal rights. If the primary route fails, the system may fail over only to another route already authorised for the same content and territory.

## Territory gate

Every programme/feed must have a rights decision before activation:

- territory
- content rights
- distribution method
- licence/agreement reference
- regulatory review status where applicable
- effective and expiry dates

Default: `REVIEW_REQUIRED`.

## Commercial/editorial separation

Sponsorship, advertising, production fees or partnership payments do not create editorial approval or guaranteed airtime.

## Operational principle

**OWN THE ENGINE. RESPECT THE LAW. MAXIMISE LAWFUL REACH. NEVER DEPEND ON ONE ROUTE.**
