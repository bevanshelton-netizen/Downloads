# YHVH GOSPEL TV — GLOBAL CHANNEL DISTRIBUTION & CONTENT PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT

**Agreement ID:** [YHVH-AGR-XXXX]

**Effective Date:** [DATE]

This Global Channel Distribution & Content Partnership Agreement (the **Agreement**) is entered into between:

**IZAKHONO AFRICA (PTY) LTD**, acting through the YHVH GOSPEL TV network (**YHVH**), and

**[PARTNER LEGAL NAME]**, a [JURISDICTION / ENTITY TYPE] (**Partner**).

YHVH and Partner are each a **Party** and together the **Parties**.

> **Legal-use notice:** This is a master commercial/operational template, not jurisdiction-specific legal advice. Before signature, the Parties should obtain appropriate legal review for the selected territories, rights, regulatory requirements, tax treatment, privacy obligations and governing law.

## 1. PURPOSE

The Parties wish to cooperate to distribute, exchange, license, produce, promote or broadcast Christian/Gospel programming and related media so that audiences can access Gospel content through lawful television, satellite, cable, FAST/CTV, OTT, mobile, web and other agreed channels.

The Parties intend the relationship to support Gospel distribution while maintaining editorial integrity, rights compliance, audience trust and safeguarding.

## 2. PARTNERSHIP MODEL

The applicable model(s) are selected in **Schedule A**:

- Channel distribution
- Content exchange
- Programme licensing
- Simulcast
- Co-production
- Event broadcast
- Digital distribution
- Translation/localisation

Unless Schedule A expressly states otherwise, the relationship is **non-exclusive**.

## 3. GRANT OF RIGHTS

Each Party grants only the rights expressly identified in Schedule A and the applicable content/rights schedule.

Every rights grant must specify:

1. territory;
2. language(s);
3. media/platform(s);
4. programme/content titles;
5. permitted formats;
6. start and end dates;
7. exclusivity, if any;
8. permitted edits, translations and promotional uses;
9. whether catch-up/VOD/short-form/social clips are permitted; and
10. whether sublicensing is permitted.

No Party grants worldwide rights merely because a programme is available online or internationally.

## 4. OWNERSHIP

Unless a definitive written acquisition or co-production agreement states otherwise, each Party retains ownership of its pre-existing intellectual property, trademarks, recordings, programmes, music, artwork, footage, formats and other materials.

A licence under this Agreement does not transfer ownership.

## 5. RIGHTS, CLEARANCES AND WARRANTIES

The Party supplying content warrants that, to the best of its knowledge and authority, it has obtained the rights and permissions necessary for the agreed uses, including applicable music, performance, performer, image, footage, location, trademark and third-party clearances.

The supplying Party must promptly notify the other Party if a rights restriction, claim, takedown demand or clearance problem arises.

## 6. EDITORIAL INDEPENDENCE

Neither payment, sponsorship, donation, commercial relationship nor distribution status purchases editorial approval, airtime, favourable treatment, ministry endorsement or guaranteed scheduling.

YHVH retains editorial and brand authority over YHVH-controlled programming, subject to definitive contractual commitments.

Partner retains editorial authority over Partner-controlled programming except to the extent a specific licence requires compliance with agreed technical, legal, safeguarding or platform standards.

## 7. CHRISTIAN / GOSPEL POSITIONING

Content supplied for YHVH distribution must be consistent with the agreed Christian/Gospel positioning and applicable editorial standards.

The Parties will respect lawful denominational and cultural differences and will not use the relationship to harass, degrade or target people on the basis of denomination, ethnicity, nationality or culture.

## 8. SAFEGUARDING

Content involving children or vulnerable persons must comply with applicable safeguarding, consent, privacy and exploitation-prevention requirements.

Neither Party may knowingly distribute content that exploits a child or vulnerable person.

## 9. HEALTH, TESTIMONY AND FINANCIAL CLAIMS

Faith testimony is permitted. Medical, mental-health, financial, fundraising or other material claims must not be knowingly deceptive or presented in a manner that violates applicable law or agreed editorial standards.

Nothing in the Agreement requires an audience member to abandon appropriate professional medical, legal or financial advice.

## 10. TECHNICAL DELIVERY

The supplying Party will deliver media in the technical specifications in Schedule B, including agreed resolution, frame rate, audio configuration, captions/subtitles, metadata, artwork and delivery mechanism.

A Party may reject or request correction of technically defective material before broadcast.

## 11. TRANSLATION AND LOCALISATION

Where translation, dubbing or subtitles are authorised, the responsible Party will use reasonable care to preserve the original meaning, Scripture references, attribution, speaker identity and material context.

## 12. DISTRIBUTION

Distribution routes may include terrestrial television, satellite, cable, IPTV, OTT, FAST/CTV, smart-TV applications, mobile applications, websites, social platforms and other agreed services.

Each Party remains responsible for obtaining licences and regulatory permissions applicable to the distribution activity it controls.

## 13. REGULATORY COMPLIANCE

Each Party will comply with laws and binding regulatory requirements applicable to its activities, including media, broadcasting, advertising, sanctions, privacy, consumer protection, intellectual property, tax and content rules.

Where a territory requires a local licence, carriage approval or other regulatory permission, activation in that territory is conditional on obtaining it.

## 14. COMMERCIAL TERMS

Any licence fee, production fee, sponsorship fee, advertising revenue share, distribution fee, minimum guarantee, expense allocation, tax treatment, currency and payment cycle must be stated in Schedule C or another signed commercial schedule.

No commercial term is implied by this Agreement.

## 15. REPORTING

Where revenue, carriage, audience, usage or other reporting is contractually required, each Party will provide the agreed reports at the agreed frequency.

Neither Party may represent unverified audience figures as guaranteed or actual results.

## 16. BRAND AND TRADEMARKS

Each Party grants limited permission to use its approved names, logos and programme marks solely for the agreed partnership and promotional purposes.

Brand assets must follow the applicable brand guidelines.

## 17. PROMOTION

The Parties may announce the partnership after signature and may use approved programme descriptions, logos and promotional materials.

Neither Party may imply an endorsement, ownership relationship or theological position that the other Party has not expressly approved.

## 18. DATA PROTECTION

Each Party will handle personal information according to applicable privacy law and the agreed role of each Party.

Personal data collected for partner onboarding, audience services, submissions or reporting will be used only for lawful and stated purposes.

## 19. CONFIDENTIALITY

Confidential commercial, technical, financial, rights and security information received from the other Party must be protected and used only for the partnership, subject to customary legal exceptions.

## 20. SECURITY

Neither Party will disclose stream keys, credentials, private URLs, security tokens or other access controls except to authorised personnel who require them.

Compromised credentials must be reported promptly.

## 21. INDEMNITY

Each Party is responsible for claims arising from its own breach of this Agreement, unlawful conduct, infringement caused by materials it supplied, or failure to obtain rights it was responsible for obtaining, subject to applicable law and the negotiated limitations in Schedule C.

## 22. SUSPENSION

Either Party may temporarily suspend affected content or distribution where reasonably necessary because of a credible rights claim, safeguarding issue, regulatory requirement, security incident, serious contractual breach or material reputational risk.

The Parties will cooperate to investigate and restore compliant distribution where appropriate.

## 23. TERMINATION

Either Party may terminate for material breach if the breach is not cured within [30] days after written notice, unless a shorter period is reasonably required because of law, safeguarding, rights, security or regulatory urgency.

Immediate termination may apply where continued performance would be unlawful or would create a serious safeguarding, rights or security risk.

Upon termination, licences cease as stated in Schedule A, subject to any agreed wind-down, archival, residual reporting or payment obligations.

## 24. FORCE MAJEURE

Neither Party is liable for delay caused by events beyond reasonable control, including major network failures, satellite failures, natural disasters, war, civil unrest, government action, regulatory restrictions or widespread infrastructure outages, provided the affected Party gives reasonable notice and mitigates the impact.

## 25. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

The Parties will first attempt good-faith commercial resolution through designated representatives.

If unresolved, the Parties will use the dispute-resolution process and governing law stated in Schedule D.

## 26. NO PARTNERSHIP ENTITY

Unless expressly stated otherwise, this Agreement does not create a joint venture, employment relationship, agency, franchise or separate legal entity.

Neither Party may bind the other without written authority.

## 27. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLICENSING

Neither Party may assign or sublicense material rights under this Agreement except as expressly permitted in Schedule A or with written consent, subject to any lawful corporate restructuring exception stated there.

## 28. ENTIRE AGREEMENT AND AMENDMENTS

This Agreement and its signed schedules constitute the agreement between the Parties concerning its subject matter and supersede prior discussions concerning that subject matter.

Amendments must be in writing and accepted by authorised representatives of both Parties.
