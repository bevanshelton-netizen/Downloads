## Verified product claims used in this pack
- KORA Stream Africa publicly presents African films, series, music, documentaries, audiobooks and creator-led channels.
- The public website invites visitors to create a free account and says no card details are required.
- KORA has a main and Kids profile experience with parent-managed controls; only approved family content appears in Kora Kids.
- Creators and rights holders can enquire/submit work; publication is subject to rights, quality and platform review.
- KORA does not guarantee audience reach, views or creator earnings. Do not imply otherwise.
- Do not advertise live channels, real-time live ad insertion, public advertiser self-service, paid campaigns, or global availability as operational until verified in production.

## Suggested launch sequence
1. Master announcement on Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn.
2. Vertical trailer on TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels and YouTube Shorts.
3. Full launch film on YouTube, embedded on KORA's website where the video asset is approved.
4. Creator recruitment post 24–48 hours later.
5. Advertiser/brand partnership post 24–48 hours after creator post.
6. WhatsApp opt-in broadcast and email to existing permissioned contacts.
7. Repeat with real catalogue highlights and creator spotlights, only where promotional rights are confirmed.

## Creative direction for the master film and cutdowns
- Master film: 60–90 seconds, 16:9.
- Social cutdowns: 15–30 seconds, 9:16 and 1:1.
- Open with a strong human/cultural moment; move through varied African stories, music, landscapes, creators and families; end with KORA brand and clear URL.
- On-screen copy: “KORA — WE ARE HERE”; “CREATE • SHARE • EARN”; “AFRICA TO THE WORLD”; “Africa's Stories. One Home.”
- Use only licensed/owned footage, music, artwork, performer releases and approved programme extracts. Do not use AI-generated scenes that imply real KORA programmes or talent.
- Ensure subtitles and readable captions; export mobile-first variants.

## Publishing checklist
- Confirm official KORA social accounts and administrator access.
- Confirm media asset URLs and usage rights.
- Confirm the destination URL and enquiry inbox are working.
- Confirm each post's tracking parameters and analytics.
- Verify the landing experience and account registration on mobile.
- Ensure comments/messages are monitored and have a response owner.
- Do not publish from a personal/other-company social brand as if it were KORA.
