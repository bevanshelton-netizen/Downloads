# NexTradeFinX V16 — Beta Operations

The controlled beta must expand slowly and reversibly.

Cohort sequence:
1. Internal owner smoke test.
2. One external learner.
3. Five-learner closed cohort.
4. Ten-learner review cohort only after a formal go/no-go.

Freeze new invites when:
- any high-severity incident is unresolved;
- support backlog exceeds three unresolved learner-impacting items;
- behavioural-risk alerts exceed review tolerance;
- disclosures/consent versions are stale.

Halt the beta immediately on:
- cross-user data access;
- privacy breach;
- privileged secret exposure;
- authentication bypass;
- any critical incident.

The halt is operational only; it does not enable or disable real trading because real trading is not part of the beta.
