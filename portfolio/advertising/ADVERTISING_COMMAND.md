# IZAKHONO Advertising Command

## Mission
Turn approved IZAKHONO products and businesses into measurable acquisition systems.

## Funnel
AD → LANDING PAGE → LEAD → QUALIFY → DEMO/QUOTE → PAYMENT → ONBOARDING → RETENTION → UPSELL → REFERRAL

## Channel allocation
- Meta: broad reach, demand creation, retargeting.
- Google: active search intent and demand capture.
- LinkedIn: high-value B2B decision-maker targeting.
- YouTube: demonstrations, education and category creation.

## Campaign data contract
Every campaign records:
- business/product
- market/country
- audience
- offer
- creative variant
- channel
- spend
- impressions/clicks
- leads
- qualified leads
- opportunities
- customers
- verified cash collected
- gross margin
- CAC
- ROAS
- retention/renewal

## Rules
1. No campaign is called successful from reach alone.
2. Projected revenue is never reported as verified revenue.
3. A payment counts only when the provider event is verified.
4. Campaigns with poor economics are paused or redesigned.
5. Winners are scaled only after conversion and cash data support scaling.
6. Localisation is required for international expansion: language, currency, payment method, market proposition and compliance.

## 30-day launch sequence
### Week 1 — Instrument
Landing pages, analytics, conversion events, CRM fields, campaign naming and verified-payment attribution.

### Week 2 — Test
Launch small creative and audience sets across Meta/Google; LinkedIn only for high-value B2B; YouTube for demonstrations.

### Week 3 — Optimise
Kill weak creative, improve offers and landing pages, retarget engaged visitors, qualify leads faster.

### Week 4 — Scale
Increase spend only on campaigns producing qualified pipeline and verified cash at acceptable economics.

## Compliance
Advertising must be truthful, substantiated and appropriate to the market. Do not imply regulatory approval, completed transactions, guaranteed outcomes, customer counts or live infrastructure unless verified.
