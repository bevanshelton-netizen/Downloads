# KORA Expansion Engine

## Objective
Turn a prospective media/content organisation into a rights-first expansion plan and, after approval, a provisionable KORA channel/business.

## Partner Intake
- Organisation and legal entity
- Contact and role
- Existing channel/platform
- Content catalogue and formats
- Audience and current distribution
- Territories controlled
- Rights ownership/licences and evidence
- Licence terms and expiry dates
- Desired model: FAST, OTT, VOD, live, radio, premium, hybrid
- Desired devices: web, Android, iOS, Smart TV, connected TV
- Monetisation: advertising, sponsorship, subscription, PPV, channel SaaS
- Launch target and commercial objectives

## Compliance Gate
1. Identify rights holder.
2. Verify licence/permission evidence.
3. Check territory and permitted platforms/use.
4. Check licence term and expiry.
5. Classify content and age suitability.
6. Assess privacy, consumer, advertising and platform obligations.
7. Determine whether the proposed service requires regulatory/legal review in each launch market.
8. Block publication when mandatory evidence is missing.

## Expansion Scoring
Score each opportunity 0-5 for:
- Audience potential
- Content depth
- Rights clarity
- Monetisation potential
- Strategic fit
- Launch readiness
- International expansion potential

A low rights/compliance score is a blocker regardless of commercial score.

## Output
Each qualified partner receives:
- Expansion score
- Rights gap report
- Recommended KORA product
- Channel architecture
- Content and scheduling plan
- Monetisation model
- Technology/provisioning plan
- Regulatory/legal review checklist
- Launch milestones
- Commercial proposal

## Provisioning Path
QUALIFIED → RIGHTS CLEARED → COMMERCIAL APPROVED → CHANNEL CONFIGURED → CONTENT INGESTED → SCHEDULED → QA → LAUNCH → ANALYTICS → MONETISATION → RENEWAL/EXPANSION

## Non-Negotiable Rule
KORA provides technology and distribution infrastructure. It does not grant rights to third-party content. No verified rights record means no publication.

## South Africa Regulatory Posture
ICASA's OTT regulatory process is evolving. KORA must not represent a partner as licensed or exempt without appropriate legal/regulatory verification. See the current ICASA OTT inquiry materials before each South African launch decision.
