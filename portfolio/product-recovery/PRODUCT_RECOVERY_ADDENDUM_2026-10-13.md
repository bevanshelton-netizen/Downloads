# IZAKHONO Product Recovery Addendum — 13 October 2026

## Purpose

This addendum records additional business/product concepts recovered from the prior picture/archive evidence search. These are additions to the permanent Product/IP Discovery Layer and must not be treated as proof of live operations, revenue, licensing or regulatory approval.

## Newly recovered business architecture

### 1. IZAKHONO MOBILITY

**Position:** asset ownership + mobility empowerment platform.

Recovered business-plan evidence describes a model that helps delivery riders move from renting motorcycles to owning productive assets. The proposed stack combines vehicle finance facilitation, rider development, fleet management, technology and entrepreneurship support.

**Pilot concept:** first 10 riders; illustrative capital requirement about R500,000; motorcycles, safety equipment, delivery boxes, tracking, insurance and working capital.

**Revenue concepts:** financing margin, fleet services, insurance commissions, training and rider apparel. Any financing/insurance/payment activity must be structured through appropriately authorised partners before launch.

**Technology:** online application, document upload, affordability assessment, repayment management, asset tracking, rider management and reporting.

**Strategic fit:** strong integration with IZAKHONO Africa manufacturing, BEVAN SHELTON apparel, Edu-Build training and the broader Opportunity Economy.

**Priority:** P1/P2 pilot subject to finance, insurance, credit, consumer-law and partner gates.

## 2. IZAKHONO OPPORTUNITY ECONOMY™

**Position:** national economic participation platform.

Recovered evidence describes an integrated pathway from **learning → earning → ownership → entrepreneurship**, combining Edu-Build Institute, IZAKHONO Earn, IZAKHONO Mobility, IZAKHONO Media Network, IZAKHONO AI and IZAKHONO Capital.

**Core proposition:** build practical pathways that allow participants to acquire skills, generate income, acquire productive assets, start businesses and eventually create employment.

**Pilot concept:** approximately 100 participants; illustrative R5 million pilot funding requirement; productive assets plus technology, operations, marketing and working capital.

**Priority:** P1 strategic programme, with individual components built and validated separately before full ecosystem claims are made.

## 3. IZAKHONO EARN

**Position:** income/opportunity layer within the Opportunity Economy.

**Purpose:** connect participants to legitimate income-generation pathways, work, enterprise opportunities and productive activity.

**Next gate:** define the first verified earning pathways, customer/employer supply, participant eligibility, payout model and measurable earnings evidence.

**Priority:** P1, but no earnings claims until actual participant transactions are verified.

## 4. IZAKHONO MEDIA NETWORK

**Position:** owned media and audience infrastructure within the Opportunity Economy.

**Purpose:** connect creators, brands, communities and participants through digital media infrastructure, with potential advertising, subscription and partnership revenue.

**Strategic integration:** KORA TV / KORA FAST / Creator TV / KORA Expansion Network.

**Priority:** P1/P2. Avoid duplicate platform development where KORA already provides the required media infrastructure.

## 5. IZAKHONO AI

**Position:** shared AI capability layer across the IZAKHONO ecosystem.

**Purpose:** provide common AI services for business automation, assessment, decision support, risk intelligence, content creation and ecosystem operations.

**Strategic integration:** IZAKHONO ONE, Idea Foundry, CashFlow Guardian, FORTRESS, Project Factory and future products.

**Priority:** P2 strategic infrastructure. Prefer shared services over isolated AI products where practical.

## 6. IZAKHONO CAPITAL

**Position:** capital layer supporting productive assets, entrepreneurship and scalable businesses.

**Purpose:** create compliant funding pathways for participants and portfolio businesses.

**Regulatory rule:** do not market as a bank, investment manager, lender, payment institution or other regulated financial entity unless the required permissions, licences, registrations and partner structures exist.

**Priority:** P3/P4 until the legal structure and regulated partner pathway are established.

## SHELTON Motor Company — evidence consolidation

The recovered SHELTON engineering programme confirms a multi-family vehicle architecture consisting of S-Series passenger cars, X-Series SUVs, B-Series/XR bakkie, Z-Series people mover, Shelton Liners and Sovereign VIP/amphibious coach. Existing engineering packs are programme-definition evidence, not production release. The programme explicitly calls for independent engineering, testing, homologation and financeable physical prototypes before production claims.

The current funding path should therefore remain milestone-gated: capital strategy → engineering/homologation appointments → prototype scope freeze → demand/pricing validation → integrated financial model → site/plant concept → corporate diligence.

The recovered investor deck also proposes a customer-led industrialisation strategy, including mining, agriculture, government, security, rental/tourism, exports, after-sales and fleet software. These are target markets, not secured orders.

## BEVAN SHELTON / BEVAN SHELTON Golf — evidence consolidation

The recovered investor material confirms the fashion house is structured as a long-term premium brand with Signature, Street, Royal Fit, Tha Top Cat, Tha Top Dawg Kiddies and BEVAN SHELTON Golf divisions.

The Golf programme is broader than apparel and includes equipment, accessories, bags/travel, footwear, corporate golf, fitting and a planned Golf House. Supplier controls include technical packs, golden samples, private-label specifications, pre-shipment QC and traceability. Partnership and revenue percentages shown in investor material remain planning assumptions until agreements and sales evidence exist.
