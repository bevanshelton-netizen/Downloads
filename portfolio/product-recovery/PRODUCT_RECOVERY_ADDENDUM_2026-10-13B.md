# IZAKHONO Product Recovery Addendum B — SHELTONAIR

## SHELTONAIR recovered operating architecture

The library contains a dedicated August 2026 SHELTONAIR investor business plan. It defines SHELTONAIR as a proposed South African integrated aviation and logistics group that launches **cargo-first** and can later expand into passenger operations.

The proposed model combines:

- scheduled and charter air freight;
- first-mile national pickup and delivery;
- line-haul trucking and airport feeder services;
- secure/high-value cargo;
- Dangerous Goods capability subject to approvals;
- pharmaceutical and temperature-controlled logistics;
- mining-critical freight;
- warehousing and cross-docking;
- customs coordination;
- specialist express and international services; and
- SHELTONAIR ONE as the digital customer/control platform.

## SHELTONAIR revenue architecture

The recovered plan identifies multiple revenue engines around one customer shipment: scheduled air freight, charter/ACMI, pickup and delivery, line-haul road transport, secure cargo, Dangerous Goods, pharma/cold chain, warehousing/handling, forwarding/customs coordination and later technology services. The commercial objective is an integrated door-to-door service rather than dependence on aircraft yield alone. fileciteturn140file8

## SHELTONAIR priority customer verticals

The evidence identifies courier/express, e-commerce, mining, pharma/healthcare, high-value cargo, automotive/manufacturing, freight forwarding, perishables, government/humanitarian work and technology/telecoms as target demand verticals. ## Regulatory gate

The business plan explicitly states that it is not an Air Service Licence, Air Operator Certificate, aircraft operating approval or Dangerous Goods authorisation. Any real operation requires the applicable licences, permits, approvals, operating specifications, training, insurance, airport permissions and commercial contracts. Financial figures are planning assumptions until independently validated. fileciteturn140file0

## Strategic route

**SHELTONAIR → cargo/logistics proof → contracted demand → regulatory/certification gates → leased/financed aircraft → network density → SHELTONAIR ONE → regional expansion → passenger readiness.**

This is therefore a **P3/P4 capital and regulatory programme**, not a claim of a currently operating airline.
