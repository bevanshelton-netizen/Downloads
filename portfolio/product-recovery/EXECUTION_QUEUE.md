# IZAKHONO Picture-Recovered Product Execution Queue

## Operating rule

Do not attempt to build every recovered product simultaneously. Convert recovered concepts into a controlled queue with one commercial lane and one strategic/R&D lane running in parallel.

## Lane A — Revenue / customer acquisition

### 1. IZAKHONO Websites + Provisioning
- Customer: startups, SMEs, organisations
- Offer: professional website + domain/SSL/email/provisioning
- Revenue: setup + monthly service
- Gate: payment + automated provisioning
- Output: customer-ready package and onboarding funnel

### 2. Meishi Digital Business Cards
- Customer: executives, salespeople, entrepreneurs, organisations
- Offer: branded digital card, NFC/QR, contact save, portfolio routing
- Revenue: once-off + subscription/team plan
- Gate: self-service creation + checkout
- Output: sellable digital product

### 3. CashFlow Guardian / SUPER ACCOUNTANT
- Customer: SMEs
- Offer: invoicing, receivables, reminders, cash-flow visibility and AI assistance
- Revenue: introduction subscription + future upsells

### 4. IZAKHONO ONE
- Customer: businesses, creators, marketing teams
- Offer: one brief → coordinated campaign assets

### 5. BEVAN SHELTON / TOP DAWG
