# IZAKHONO GLOBAL ADAPTATION ENGINE

## Mission
Adapt the IZAKHONO value-capture model to every continent without exporting assumptions from one jurisdiction into another.

## Core principle
**Build locally. Connect globally. Create value. Retain ownership. Share fairly.**

## Market adaptation gates
1. Jurisdiction and legal entity
2. Applicable sector regulation
3. Customer and market definition
4. Local language and cultural adaptation
5. Currency, tax and invoicing requirements
6. Local payment rails and settlement
7. Data protection, cybersecurity and cross-border transfer requirements
8. Consumer protection and advertising rules
9. Intellectual-property, content and licensing rights
10. Local partner/vendor requirements
11. Import/export, logistics and customs where physical goods are involved
12. Local support and dispute-resolution process

## Commercial model
Every deployment must identify:
- who creates the value;
- who owns the underlying asset/IP/content;
- what IZAKHONO provides;
- what the customer pays;
- what third parties cost;
- how revenue is shared where applicable;
- what data and analytics are available;
- how renewal, expansion and termination work.

## Expansion sequence
LOCAL PROOF → JURISDICTION CLEARANCE → COMMERCIAL MODEL → LOCAL PARTNERS → PROVISION → LAUNCH → VERIFIED REVENUE → MEASURE → REGIONAL EXPANSION → CONTINENTAL EXPANSION → GLOBAL EXPANSION

## Rights-first rule
No verified ownership, licence or permission record = no distribution of third-party protected content/assets.

## Regulatory rule
IZAKHONO does not represent a product, partner or service as licensed, approved, exempt or compliant until the relevant jurisdiction-specific evidence has been verified.

## Global market clusters
- Africa: AfCFTA-aware regional expansion, local payments, multilingual and MSME enablement.
- Europe: country/EU-specific consumer, privacy, tax, platform and sector requirements.
- North America: federal + state/provincial requirements, payments, privacy and sector rules.
- South America: country-specific tax, payments, consumer and data requirements.
- Asia: country-specific licensing, data, payments, consumer and platform requirements.
- Oceania: country-specific privacy, consumer, payments and sector requirements.

## Product architecture
The same core platform can power KORA, commerce, manufacturing, education, services, communications and other IZAKHONO businesses, while jurisdiction adapters handle local requirements.

## Success metric
Not the number of markets entered. The north-star is **verified businesses activated and generating verified customer revenue while preserving required rights, compliance evidence and transparent commercial records**.
