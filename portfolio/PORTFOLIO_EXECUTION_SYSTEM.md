## Commercial gate
A business may move from BUILD to SELL only when:
- product proposition is defined;
- customer and acquisition route are defined;
- price/revenue model exists;
- operational workflow is testable;
- applicable legal/compliance gates are identified;
- payment route is specified;
- production evidence exists.

Revenue is only marked verified after a provider-confirmed successful transaction is reconciled into the IZAKHONO revenue ledger.

## Deployment gate
Never mark LIVE merely because code exists, a deployment was requested, or a URL was generated. Require verified production state and smoke test evidence.

## Manufacturing strategy
Prioritise products that create local production, supplier development, export potential and recurring customer demand. South Africa's 2026 Industrial Development Strategy emphasises productive capacity, diversification, digitalisation and stronger value chains. Localisation is an explicit national industrial priority.

## Regulatory principle
Regulated businesses remain commercially designed but legally gated. Build the technology, operating model, partner pack, compliance evidence and customer pipeline in parallel, but do not activate regulated transactions or operations until the required licences, approvals, contracts and controls are in place.

## SHELTONAIR launch architecture
Cargo first; passenger ready. Initial value chain: pickup -> road feeder -> secure handling -> air freight/contracted capacity -> destination handling -> final delivery -> digital visibility. Specialist verticals include express, e-commerce, mining critical freight, pharma/cold chain, high-value cargo, automotive/manufacturing, freight forwarding, perishables, government/humanitarian and technology/telecoms.

The airline operating layer remains subject to SACAA licensing/AOC requirements and all other applicable aviation approvals. The current investor plan is planning material, not proof of an operating licence.
