Privacy notice — private beta

AUTO AI processes the vehicle and symptom information you submit to provide vehicle decision support. During the private beta, do not submit passwords, payment-card data, identity documents or unnecessary personal information.

Vehicle details, diagnostic descriptions and quotation text may be processed by an AI service when an approved AI gateway is configured. Before public launch, the production notice will state retention, deletion, cross-border processing and data-controller details for each launch market.

Location is not collected by this MVP. Camera and microphone access are not used by this MVP. Future photo/audio features will request explicit device permission.
