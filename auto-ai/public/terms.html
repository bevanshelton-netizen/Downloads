Beta terms & safety

AUTO AI provides educational vehicle triage and decision support. It does not replace a qualified mechanic, roadworthy inspection, manufacturer service information, vehicle-title check, insurer assessment or emergency service.

Never continue driving when braking, steering, wheel security, fuel leakage, fire, severe overheating, red oil-pressure warnings or other dangerous conditions are present. Stop in a safe place and obtain qualified assistance.

Fault codes identify detected conditions, not automatically the component that must be replaced. Confirm faults with appropriate tests before authorising expensive work.
