Privacy notice — public beta

AUTO AI processes the vehicle and symptom information you submit to provide vehicle decision support. Do not submit passwords, payment-card data, identity documents or unnecessary personal information.

During the browser beta, core safety, fault-code, quote-review and used-car screening can run locally on your device. When an approved AI service is connected, vehicle details and diagnostic descriptions may be sent to that service for deeper analysis; the production notice will identify those processing arrangements before paid launch.

Location is not collected by this beta. Camera and microphone access are not used yet. Future photo/audio features will request explicit device permission.
