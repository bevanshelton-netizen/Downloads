# IZAKHONO TRADE

Capital-light catalogue + Fit & Order Hub commerce platform.

## Included
- Responsive catalogue storefront
- Grade 1 / Grade 8 school-pack builder
- Six-school East London launch selector
- Ready Stock / Rapid Supply / Factory Direct / Pre-Order fulfilment states
- Christmas/everyday hero-product catalogue
- Cart and demo checkout
- Smart-stock and physical Fit & Order Hub explanation
- Zero external runtime dependencies

The full launch bundle is also maintained as a conversation artifact. Production payment, school-spec and supplier claims remain gated until credentials/approvals are verified.
